**Erastus 16, 4712 AR**

Sho woke slumped and tied to the mast.

Master Scourge was there, placing the whip under her chin as she came around.

> "Oh good.... You're awake."

He asked whether she had enjoyed it.

Sho groaned, then answered the grin on his face.

> "Im guessing you did from that grin on your face"

Scourge did not hide it.

> "Yes... yes I did."

Before the exchange could fully continue, Ratline's voice rang out from the rigging.

> "Land ho~!"

The call stirred the deck into commotion.

Sho asked to be let down if Scourge would allow it. Scourge said Plugg wanted to talk to her and released her chin, needling her with the line that a tough girl like her could handle it.

Then the summons widened.

Scourge ordered the lizard brought on deck.

Plugg called Harvus out of the rigging.

Rak arrived in his usual voice.

> "Sup?"

Scourge unlocked Sho and told Rak he was due up there too.

Rak seized on the word.

> "DUE!?"

Sho rubbed her wrists and stood wearily.

Elsewhere, Tam "Narwhal" Tate delivered Mirei's summons.

> "You're wanted on deck... that voice of yours doesn't get you out of everything."

Mirei headed up.

A few moments later, Plugg stood with five buckets at his feet.

Four had been gathered: Harvus, Rak, Sho, and Mirei.

Plugg then yelled for Scourge to get Badger.

When Badger was added, Plugg pointed the five toward the reef to the fore and starboard.

> "Good, good. The captain wants crabs... and I know just the lot to get them!"

Mirei sighed.

Sho sighed with her.

Rak treated it as surf.

Plugg gave the task plainly. They were to swim to the reef, fill each bucket with crabs, and swim back.

> "Am I 'clear."

Then came the order to drop anchor and get to work.

The five jumped from the Wormwood and began swimming toward the reef.

The reef was dangerous.

Reefclaws lived among the coral, and what had been presented as a simple errand for the captain's supper became a fight in the water.

By the time the group returned to the Wormwood, they carried both crabs and the larger creatures they had killed.

The crew returned with their catches one at a time.

Plugg judged Mirei's catch first.

> "That's what you brought.... they look awfly small."

Mirei muttered that they looked fine to her and went to take them below.

Plugg approved Rak's catch more strongly.

> "Hmm, good. Good. Talk to fishguts and cook them up for captain."

> "Those make fine eating."

Harvus returned with a bucket of crabs. Plugg insulted the catch as small and blamed Harvus' nature.

> "Small, I suppose it's to be expected from a bird in the water... to the gally."

In the galley, Mirei delivered her crabs with less ceremony.

> "TAKE MEH CRABS"

Kroop recognized the danger of what they had found.

> "You ran into these bastards out there huh? Can be ruff in the reefs. Were they any trouble?"

Mirei said they were nasty and had tried to eat her legs.

Rak claimed he had dropped one immediately.

> "No way brah, 1 shot that like a beast"

Sho came back later, still feeling the previous night's punishment.

Plugg asked what had taken her so long.

Sho answered plainly.

> "Hard to do some swimming after a night shackled to the mast."

She still had a full bucket.

Plugg turned the delay back into discipline.

> "Guess you'll have to mind that pretty mouth... to the gally."

After the cooking, Caulky Tarroon came to the galley. The captain wanted to know who had caught the big one.

Rak identified himself.

Kroop confirmed it.

> "Afraid it's true lass, it's my assistant."

Caulky was not pleased that it had to be Rak, but she took him and the dinner to Harrigan.

She introduced him as the source of dinner.

Rak greeted the cabin with his usual lack of ceremony.

> "Sup?"

Harrigan accepted the result.

> "You've done well, I've instructed Grok to give you anything we took from you from our stores... remember, I smile on those who go above and beyond!"

Rak took that as good news.

Scourge sneered.

Riaris Krine wanted the food brought on and asked whether the reefclaw had been feisty.

> "Bring on the grub~! Was she feisty?"

Caulky shook her head at Rak.

> "He doesn't really say much that makes sense."

Rak described the fight in his own way.

> "Nah, I gave her the ol' one two like wham-bam and she went down like sploosh"

Caulky cut and tasted the food, swallowed slowly, and nearly choked at the phrasing.

> "...jakenape...."

Riaris did not care about the strange delivery.

> "Good enough for me! Dig in."

Harrigan checked on Caulky, who insisted she was fine and excused herself.

Harrigan reinforced the lesson for Rak.

> "Do right by me lizard and I'll treat you right!"

> "Theirs money in it for you, and infamy too!"

Scourge glared.

Plugg dismissed Rak with a rum warning.

> "You... are dismissed, don't let me catch you throwing that rum out."

Scourge muttered that he hoped for the chance.

Peppery Longfarthing observed the obvious.

> "What a strange creature."

Riaris reduced the matter to the practical outcome.

> "Caught good food, good enough for me."

That night, Sandara found Harvus and asked how the reef had gone.

She had asked Scourge if she could come with them, but he refused.

> "I asked that ass Scourge if I could come with... guess he still holds a grudge."

Harvus slouched before her in pain.

Sandara immediately shifted from conversation to care and asked whether anyone else had been hurt.

Harvus named Scourge as the culprit and said Mirei had also been hurt.

> "Scourge.... that apish geck."

Sandara decided they should gather the wounded.

> "Basmara's blessings will smile on them this day."

She and Harvus found Mirei and Sho.

Mirei said a monster in the sea had gotten her leg.

Sho had not realized Mirei had been fighting the reef creatures.

> "I think I saw part of one at the bottom of the reef. Didnt know you did battle with them. Sorry I wasnt there"

Sandara told them to stand still, concentrated, touched Sho and Harvus, and invoked divine aid.

Sho felt an energy pulse through her.

> "yeah, thanks, the fog in my head is lessened by quite a bit"

Sandara asked Mirei how she was and reminded all of them to save their strength and keep their friends close.

Sho and Mirei both nodded.

Harvus kissed Sandara's hand and noticed the necklace he had given her.

Sandara smiled, then softened and admitted the reason.

> "With Rosie gone, I... anyway. It's no problem."

She said she was keeping it safe.

Mirei left awkwardly. Sho also excused herself and thanked Sandara again.

Harvus then said he suddenly felt the urge to sneak around the ship.

Sandara told him good luck and not to get caught. Then she looked out to sea, touching her ebony idol.

At the quartermaster's store, Grok grinned at Rak.

Harrigan had instructed Grok to return anything taken from Rak from the ship stores, and Grok led him into the store to find what was his.

> "So you finally decided to stop fighting it huh? Well, I always liked you."

Rak insisted he had not stopped fighting anything. He said he had only seen delicious lobster, taken it for laughs, and ended up getting sweet goods for it.

Grok accepted the strangeness.

> "You know, I think you've gotten stranger every day we've talked. Still cute"

She opened the chest and rummaged through it, asking which things were his.

The conversation quickly became flirtation. Rak asked whether she was busy tonight and proposed a game called slap and tickle.

Grok had not heard of it, but asked whether he would teach her.

Rak asked whether "he" would wake up.

Grok asked how intensive the game was.

Rak said it was quite intensive.

Grok cracked her knuckles.

> "Let's see what you got!"

Rak took her up on the challenge.

Whatever possessions Harrigan had ordered returned could wait until morning. Grok led him away from the quartermaster's store and, before long, Rak retired with her to her bed.

The Wormwood settled around them.

The night watch moved across the deck overhead. Sailors climbed through the rigging. Hammocks shifted below. Timber creaked with the slow movement of the sea.

For a while, another rhythm joined it.

A muffled thump sounded through the deck.

Then another.

The boards creaked beneath Grok's quarters with considerably less regard for the ship's usual rhythm.

Anyone awake nearby was left to make of it what they would.

Rak and Grok's game of slap and tickle continued well into the night.

Around them, the Wormwood sailed on.
