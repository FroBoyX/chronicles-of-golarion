## Overview

Bonewrack Isle is the location where the campaign stops being only an adaptation of the published adventure and becomes fully character-driven.

The island begins as a survival site: food, water, missing crew, repairs, and shipboard danger.

It becomes the stage for the first true fracture of the future crew.

## Published Baseline

The Man's Promise runs aground on the coral reefs surrounding Bonewrack Isle after a storm.

The published island contains:

- C3, an abandoned fishing village on the northern beach;
- C4, a broken-bridge mire;
- C5, the ghoul-infested Whores' Boudoir;
- C6, Crab Palm Beach;
- C7, overgrown fields;
- C8, Arron Ivy's stockade and spring;
- C9, the wreck of the Infernus;
- and D, Riptide Cove.

The AP remains scaffold only where the played campaign differs.

## Campaign Version

The storm occurs on **Erastus 31, 4712 AR**. Sandara Quinn and Tilly Brackett are the initial abductees. Goyle Onorach is captured later after Plugg uses the Tidal Cutlass during another grindylow attack.

The party does not experience Bonewrack as a linear dungeon. They explore, retreat, split, return, conceal information, and create multiple simultaneous fronts.

## Played Route

The broad first-expedition route is:

1. C3 abandoned village;
2. C4 mire and broken bridge;
3. first camp between C4 and C5;
4. C5 rotting ghoul lair and later great-tree fire;
5. C6 Crab Palm Beach / Coconut Beach;
6. C7 overgrown cornfield;
7. C8 stockade, spring, and spyglass;
8. D Riptide Cove;
9. C9 Infernus wreck sighted offshore but not explored.

## Current Island State

### C3 - Abandoned Fishing Village

The collapsed northern-beach village is the original landing area and later return point.

The path from C3 leads directly toward C4. Scourge's shore party later searches the village, and Rak and Sho return here before their confrontation with Scourge.

### C4 - The Mire

The played broken crossing is the AP mire:

- roughly 70 feet of salt river, bog, and quicksand;
- remnants of an old bridge at either bank;
- pilings through the mud;
- overhanging branches;
- two giant frogs concealed in the mire.

Rak's failed `PARKOUR` jump across the pilings becomes a repeated route marker. Mirei uses magic to disable or entrance the frogs long enough for the party to escape.

No first-expedition frog kill is confirmed. The later supply party encounters frogs at the same hazard, so one or both original frogs likely survived.

### First Camp

The first camp occurs after C4 and before C5.

Mirei hides an alchemist's-fire flask during this camp. Three undead women attack during the night.

### C5 - Whores' Boudoir / Rotting Camp

C5 is the foul tree-and-tent site later burned into the `great tree fire` landmark.

The AP gives C5 exactly three ghoul women who leave their lair at night to hunt the island paths. This strongly identifies them as the three first-night attackers.

Their exact casualties remain uncertain. The record does not establish how many died at camp, returned to C5, or moved elsewhere.

The party later reaches and burns the rotting site. The fire becomes the post-Riptide camp and a route landmark used by Rak's escort branch.

The AP places six alchemist's-fire flasks and other treasure at C5, but no recovery is established. Mirei's earlier hidden flask is a separate object unless later evidence proves otherwise.

### C6 - Crab Palm Beach / Coconut Beach

**Crab Palm Beach** is the AP name. **Coconut Beach** is the played/archive name for the same location.

The AP places three giant crabs in separate coconut palms. The played scene prominently activates one crab, which drops onto and kills a lone undead figure.

The AP does not place an undead creature at C6. The figure is therefore campaign-specific. It may have been an escaped C5 ghoul, but that is not locked.

Sho and Harvus gather coconuts while Rak makes a carrying basket. The injury-driven Arodus 2 recovery occurs at the beach or nearby rocky slopes.

Fipps is later left here by Syl to await the Man's Promise.

### C7 - The Fields

The overgrown cornfield lies south of C6 and east of C8.

Rak later uses the spyglass to inspect it from the route fork. The first party does not fully explore or clear it. Harvus and Mirei skirt it after the rescue.

### C8 - Castaway's Stockade

The party finds:

- the timber stockade;
- the island's only confirmed fresh-water spring;
- two vine chokers in the great tree;
- Arron Ivy hanging inside the lodge;
- a botfly-infested interior hazard;
- and a weathered spyglass fixed toward Riptide Cove.

Rak's report preserves the vine creatures and hanged inhabitant but not a completed combat or looting sequence. Do not assume the chokers, Ivy, or botfly swarm were destroyed.

Rak fills his waterskin at the spring and later reports the water source to Scourge.

#### First tricorne sighting

The fixed spyglass shows two grindylows in D1, one wearing Sandara's tricorne. Rak's account independently says he saw grindylows from the stronghold wearing items connected to the ship attack.

The strongest sequence is:

1. first sighting through the C8 spyglass;
2. tricorne worn by a grindylow in D3 during the first assault;
3. hat-wearing grindylow present in D8 during the rescue;
4. hat carrier escapes with the Queen.

#### Rak's spyglass

Rak later calls it his `spiffy new spyglass` and uses it at the fork to inspect the cornfield.

The strongest reconstruction is that he removed the C8 spyglass and retained it. This does not imply that the other C8 treasure was taken.

### C9 - Wreck of the Infernus

The wreck lies offshore southwest of the island.

Rak, Sho, and Harvus support sighting a wreck from the cliff or open-sea side of Riptide Cove. Nobody investigates it during the preserved sequence.

### D - Riptide Cove

The first assault route is:

- D1 cliff cove;
- D3 Fronds entry;
- retreat back through D1;
- escape through D2 Neck to the open sea.

The second assault begins from the sea through D2 and returns through D1 and D3 toward D8.

Campaign-specific cave facts:

- D4 was avoided;
- five D3 grindylow deaths are recorded by position;
- no D7 grindylow casualties are recorded;
- the devilfish was moved into D8 by the GM;
- Goyle was added as a third captive;
- a summoned octopus died in the Cauldron;
- the Whale charged Harvus from below during Harvus's descent;
- Harvus braced Rak's spear and killed the Whale with an actual critical hit and triple damage;
- the corpse shielded the continued descent and diverted the devilfish;
- Harvus freed Tilly, Sandara, and Goyle;
- the remaining grindylows fled;
- Mirei witnessed the Queen and hat-wearing grindylow escape.

Nobody returned to Riptide Cove after the rescue.

## Consequences Created by Play

### Harvus Becomes a Legend Candidate

Harvus's actual critical hit against the Whale is the first event likely to become pirate folklore if told well.

### Mirei Chooses Not to Betray

Mirei had the opportunity to act on Plugg's command and did not. Her confession changes her trust position.

### Rak Becomes Practical Leader Under Hostile Eyes

Rak's island knowledge makes him useful even to enemies. His lost spear materially ties him to Harvus's Whale kill.

### Sho Opens a Separate Front

Sho's imprisonment gives her direct access to Man's Promise survivor grievances and potential anti-Plugg sentiment.

### The Brinebrood Queen Survives

The Queen's retreat transforms the monster threat into a future vendetta.
