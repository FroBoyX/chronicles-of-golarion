# Bonewrack Isle

## Document Status

- Type: Book One location and relaunch state document
- Maturity: Working, reconciled through Riptide Cove map and GM clarification
- Scope: Bonewrack Isle as published, modified, and played
- Cave control: `chronology/bonewrack-riptide-cove-map-and-item-reconstruction.md`

## Overview

Bonewrack Isle is the location where the campaign stops being only an adaptation of the published adventure and becomes fully character-driven.

The island begins as a survival site: food, water, missing crew, repairs, and shipboard danger.

It becomes the stage for the first true fracture of the future crew.

## Published Baseline

The Man's Promise runs aground on the coral reefs surrounding Bonewrack Isle after a storm.

The published island contains:

- an abandoned fishing village;
- a swamp and mire;
- ghoul-fever-infested insects;
- the remains of the Infernus;
- Crab Palm Beach;
- overgrown fields;
- Arron Ivy's stockade and spring;
- and Riptide Cove, home of the Brinebrood grindylows.

The published adventure expects the PCs to gather supplies, rescue Sandara and another captive, then return to resolve the mutiny.

The AP remains scaffold only where the played campaign differs.

## Campaign Version

The campaign version adds additional consequence and specificity.

The storm occurs on **Erastus 31, 4712 AR**. Sandara Quinn and Tilly Brackett are the initial abductees. Goyle Onorach is captured later after Plugg uses the Tidal Cutlass during another grindylow attack.

The party did not experience Bonewrack as a linear dungeon. They explored, retreated, split, returned, concealed information, and created multiple simultaneous fronts.

## Current Island State

### Abandoned Fishing Village

The original landing area and a key return point.

Scourge's shore party later operates here while searching for supplies. Rak and Sho return here before reaching the Man's Promise.

### The Mire

A dangerous crossing with broken bridge remnants and frog threats.

Rak's failed `PARKOUR` crossing becomes a repeated route marker. Rak later guides Syl, Shivikah, Jape, and Fipps through the same general hazard.

### Whores' Boudoir / Rotting Camp

A diseased, ghoul-haunted camp tied to the Infernus castaways.

The party burns the site. The fire becomes the `great tree fire` landmark and the later post-Riptide camp.

### Coconut / Crab Palm Beach

The party discovers coconut palms, the lone undead figure, and the giant crab hazard.

Coconuts become a supply objective. The injury-driven Arodus 2 recovery occurs in the beach / nearby rocky-slope area.

Fipps is later left at Coconut Beach by Syl to await the ship.

### The Fields

An overgrown cornfield discovered but not fully investigated.

It remains potentially valuable as food and dangerous because of poor visibility and unknown hazards.

### Castaway Stockade

The party finds the stockade, spring, vine creatures, hanged inhabitant, and spyglass.

The spring is the only confirmed fresh-water source on Bonewrack.

The tricorne is associated with a grindylow and helps direct Harvus toward Riptide Cove.

### Riptide Cove

The grindylow lair.

The first assault route is:

- D1 cliff cove;
- D3 Fronds entry;
- retreat back through D1;
- escape through D2 Neck to the open sea.

The second assault route begins from the sea through D2 and returns inward through D1 and D3 toward D8.

Campaign-specific cave facts:

- D4 was avoided;
- five D3 grindylow deaths are recorded by position;
- no D7 grindylow casualties are recorded;
- the devilfish was moved into D8 by the GM;
- Goyle was added as a third captive;
- a summoned octopus died in the Cauldron;
- the Whale charged Harvus from below during Harvus's descent;
- Harvus braced Rak's spear and killed the Whale with an actual critical hit and triple damage;
- the Whale's corpse shielded the continued descent and diverted the devilfish;
- Harvus then freed Tilly, Sandara, and Goyle;
- the remaining grindylows fled;
- Mirei witnessed the Queen and hat-wearing grindylow escape.

Nobody returned to Riptide Cove after the rescue.

## Current Riptide Item State

- Sandara does not have the tricorne; the hat carrier escaped with the Queen.

## Known But Not Fully Resolved

### Wreck of the Infernus

The wreck was sighted offshore but not explored.

It remains a possible future source of Chelish evidence, treasure, disease context, and historical threads.

### Remaining Grindylows

Riptide Cove's organized defense is broken after the Whale's death and Queen's retreat.

Some grindylows escaped with the Queen or scattered. Exact surviving count remains open.

### Brinebrood Queen

The Queen survived and fled with her weapon and the hat-wearing grindylow.

Mirei witnessed the flight. The Queen has a personal reason to hate Harvus, who killed the Whale.

This remains a live future hook.

## Consequences Created by Play

### Harvus Becomes a Legend Candidate

Harvus's actual critical hit against the Whale is the first event likely to become pirate folklore if told well.

### Mirei Chooses Not to Betray

Mirei had the opportunity to act on Plugg's command and did not. Her confession changes her trust position.

### Rak Becomes Practical Leader Under Hostile Eyes

Rak's island knowledge makes him useful even to enemies. His lost spear materially ties him to Harvus's Whale kill.

### Sho Opens a Separate Front

Sho's imprisonment gives her direct access to Man's Promise survivor grievances and potential anti-Plugg sentiment.

### The Brinebrood Queen Survives

The Queen's retreat transforms the monster threat into a future vendetta.
