# Bonewrack Island Memory / Chronology Spine — Arodus 1-4

## Document Status

- Type: GM-side source spine / memory reconstruction
- Maturity: Working
- Scope: Erastus 31 storm context, first Bonewrack landing on Arodus 1, island events during Arodus 1-4, and the rescue endpoint immediately before the Arodus 5 split branches
- Source basis: recovered table fragments, sanitized session-log summaries, current GM corrections, Harvus' preserved player notes, later player-memory answers, existing campaign reconstruction, and Adventure Path scaffold
- Spoiler scope: GM-only / character-limited
- Canon status: Canon-candidate sequence with explicit source boundaries and unresolved internal dating
- Narrative status: Not polished narrative; do not use as player-facing prose without filtering
- Arodus 5 rule: This file stops at the handoff into the existing Arodus 5 branches. It does not restage the fork, Sho's hot-box conversation, or Rak's later supply party.

## Purpose

This file reconstructs the Bonewrack Island sequence without forcing the surviving evidence into a complete day-by-day chronology.

The island evidence supports a strong relative sequence, but only a few calendar anchors are secure:

- the storm and initial abductions occur on Erastus 31, 4712 AR;
- the Man's Promise grounds on the reef and the first island party lands on Arodus 1, 4712 AR;
- the party remains on or around Bonewrack for three or more days;
- the successful Riptide Cove rescue is complete immediately before the preserved Arodus 5 rescue-side scene begins.

Intermediate events should remain in relative order until additional logs, GM rulings, or player-specific memory establish exact placement.

## Neutral Chronology Spine

### Beat 0 — Storm Crisis and Open Grindylow Fight

**Calendar anchor:** Erastus 31, 4712 AR. Context immediately before the island sequence.

**Confirmed table / GM-locked events**

- A major storm drives the Man's Promise southwest and forces all hands into emergency work. (`GM-LOCK`)
- Grindylows use the storm to take Tilly Brackett and Sandara Quinn before attacking openly. (`GM-LOCK`, supported by `AP-SCAFFOLD` structure)
- Harvus is in the rigging during part of the open fight. (`TABLE-DIRECT`)
- Sho and Rak fight grindylows on deck; Rak is reduced to 2 of 20 hit points before receiving healing. (`TABLE-DIRECT`)
- Mirei falls from the rigging, and Sho moves under her to cushion or catch the fall. (`TABLE-DIRECT`)
- Below decks, the galley and starboard side are damaged and water is entering the ship. (`TABLE-SUMMARY`)

**Player-note additions**

- Harvus remembers later going below to help bail water and feeling rage toward Syl. (`PLAYER-NOTE`)

**Unresolved**

- Whether Harvus moved from rigging to bailing after the preserved fight fragment or whether the note compresses separate storm phases.
- When the crew first realized Sandara and Tilly were gone.
- Which NPCs were visible during the open fight.

### Beat 1 — Reef Grounding, Damage Assessment, and Missing Crew

**Calendar anchor:** Arodus 1, 4712 AR.

**Confirmed table / GM-locked events**

- The Man's Promise is blown onto a reef beside Bonewrack Isle. (`GM-LOCK`)
- The ship's fresh water is gone or unusable. (`GM-LOCK`, `TABLE-SUMMARY`)
- Repairs are expected to require roughly a day and a half to two days. (`GM-LOCK`, `TABLE-SUMMARY`; preserve the range rather than choosing one phrasing)
- Tilly and Sandara remain missing after the storm. (`GM-LOCK`)

**Player-note additions**

- Harvus remembers Plugg calling roll, Sandara being missing, and searching the deck with Rak without finding her. (`PLAYER-NOTE`)
- Rak gives Harvus Sandara's holy symbol before the island search. Harvus later reads the gesture as trust; Rak's player remembers the choice as practical. (`PLAYER-NOTE`, `PLAYER-MEMORY`)

**AP scaffold only**

- The published version places a hole in the starboard main hold near the water barrel, requires constant pumping, and allows Sandara's dropped holy symbol to be found on deck. Do not import those exact details where the campaign evidence differs. (`AP-SCAFFOLD`)

**Unresolved**

- Exact roll-call timing relative to the repair assessment and Plugg's confrontation with Mirei.
- Whether Sandara's holy symbol was found exactly as in the AP, recovered another way, or already held by Rak through a table-specific sequence.

### Beat 2 — Plugg's Island Assignment and Mirei's Hidden Loyalty Test

**Relative placement:** After the storm and damage assessment; before the first landing.

**Confirmed table events**

- Syl reports that Mirei went off mission by speaking with Sho / the monk during the prior crew-gauging assignment. (`TABLE-SUMMARY`)
- Plugg physically confronts Mirei and gives her a new whispered loyalty test. The exact words do not survive in the summary. (`TABLE-SUMMARY`)
- Plugg says the ship lacks food and water and sends Rak by rowboat with Sho, Mirei, and Harvus to seek supplies. (`TABLE-SUMMARY`)
- The later confession establishes the outer shape of Mirei's task: the expedition was intended to be one-way for most of the others, and Mirei was expected to kill or betray them to prove loyalty. (`TABLE-SUMMARY`, confirmed only by Mirei's own later statement)

**Character-limited fact**

- Mirei alone knows the exact instruction she heard and her own immediate interpretation, unless she shared part of it before the preserved rescue-side confession.

**Unresolved**

- Exact whispered wording.
- Whether Plugg named particular targets or left the method and timing to Mirei.
