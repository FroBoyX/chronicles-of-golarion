## Sho / Owlbear Thread

This scene should continue rather than restart the relationship established on Erastus 17.

Safe handling:

- Owlbear remains chained in the middle hold near the foremast.
- Sho has already begun seeing him as lonely and constrained rather than simply dangerous.
- On Erastus 18 she continues speaking to him and treats him as a person rather than an attraction or punishment tool.
- The phrase "more than a side show" is evidence-supported as the meaning of the scene, though exact words are not preserved.
- The interaction can deepen Sho's protectiveness and show that the social boundary around Owlbear is beginning to weaken from her side.

Do not:

- free Owlbear;
- imply broad crew sympathy for him without evidence;
- make Owlbear harmless;
- turn the scene into a full reconciliation with Plugg or the crew;
- repeat the entire Erastus 17 conversation about him being sad and lonely.

## Harvus / Sho Secret Scene

This is the day's strongest confirmed character beat.

Required content:

- Harvus approaches or catches Sho in a setting where the exchange can remain private enough to preserve the secret.
- He raises the subject bluntly rather than delicately.
- He states or clearly implies that he knows Sho is a woman.
- Sho reacts with immediate alarm.
- She asks him to keep it quiet.
- Her reasoning is practical: fewer people knowing is safer.
- Harvus identifies one of her whippings as the point where the truth became apparent.
- He agrees not to tell anyone.

Tone:

- Harvus should read as tactless, not predatory.
- Sho should read as guarded and alarmed rather than ashamed of being a woman.
- The danger is the loss of control over information aboard a hostile ship.
- Harvus' promise should matter because he could have used the knowledge and chooses not to.

Knowledge boundary after the scene:

- Mirei already knows or has noticed.
- Harvus now explicitly knows and has promised silence.
- Do not expand the circle of knowledge without separate evidence.
- Do not imply Scourge, Plugg, or the hostile crew understood the Erastus 15 disguise slip.
