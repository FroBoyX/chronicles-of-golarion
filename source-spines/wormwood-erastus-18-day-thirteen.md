## Sho / Owlbear Thread

This scene should continue rather than restart the relationship established on Erastus 17.

Safe handling:

- Owlbear remains chained in the middle hold near the foremast.
- Sho has already begun seeing him as lonely and constrained rather than simply dangerous.
- On Erastus 18 she continues speaking to him and treats him as a person rather than an attraction or punishment tool.
- The phrase `more than a side show` is evidence-supported as the meaning of the scene, though exact words are not preserved.
- The interaction can deepen Sho's protectiveness and show that the social boundary around Owlbear is beginning to weaken from her side.

### Voice and meaningful extension

The conversation may be meaningfully reconstructed because several independent anchors constrain both characters:

- AP and dossier handling establish Owlbear as simpleminded in presentation, abused, dangerous, accustomed to clowning because the crew laughs, and responsive to mercy.
- The locked Erastus 10 fight establishes that Owlbear understood Sho's decision not to keep hurting him and looked to Plugg in fear when he was failing.
- Later direct Owlbear speech is clipped and concrete rather than eloquent: short commands and simple questions such as asking what someone is doing, telling them to wait, and telling them to go.
- Later recovered evidence also shows that Sho eventually realizes Owlbear is wiser and more patient than she first assumed. That later realization should be foreshadowed here, not fully spent early.

Therefore:

- keep Owlbear's speech short, concrete, and observant;
- do not give him a polished speech about abuse, freedom, or morality;
- do not write him as a child or as comic stupidity;
- let Sho ask most of the questions and do most of the interpretation;
- useful topics include why he performs foolishness for the crew, what he actually likes, what he remembers about the fight with Sho, and the difference between being laughed with and laughed at;
- a small sign that Owlbear notices more than Sho expects is desirable;
- reserve the later explicit `I can break the chains / I bide my time` idea for its later recovered placement rather than moving it onto Erastus 18.

Do not:

- free Owlbear;
- imply broad crew sympathy for him without evidence;
- make Owlbear harmless;
- turn the scene into a full reconciliation with Plugg or the crew;
- repeat the entire Erastus 17 conversation about him being sad and lonely;
- make Owlbear suddenly philosophical or verbally sophisticated.

## Harvus AP-Informed Exploration After Sho

The GM has authorized an extension in which Harvus leaves Sho and uses the heat-weary ship as an opportunity to see what he can get up to.

This should be modest and exploratory, not a successful heist.

### AP structure to preserve

- Treat this as a **Sneak** ship action or equivalent narrative use of the AP's limited private-action economy.
- One primary area should receive actual exploration attention.
- Ordinary transit through other spaces does not need to be treated as separate successful searches.
- Do not grant Harvus major loot, secret documents, sabotage, or access to a locked officer room without evidence.
- The heat wave may motivate Harvus to believe people are tired and less attentive, but the AP provides no formal alertness penalty.

### Preferred route / target

The strongest target is the **officer-quarter threshold** reached from the middle hold.

Harvus can:

- leave Sho and wait until attention has shifted;
- move through the lower and middle holds under ordinary ship traffic;
- note the stairs and routes that divide crew space from officer space;
- linger near the officer-quarter access long enough to learn the rhythm of who comes and goes;
- recognize that the officer doors, locks, and boundaries are more serious than ordinary crew lockers;
- possibly hear indistinct movement or voices without learning privileged information;
- decide against forcing a lock or touching anything that looks trapped;
- retreat without being caught;
- on the way back, gain a clearer mental map of the captain-side route, galley / quartermaster side, Owlbear's position, and the vertical relation between middle hold, lower hold, and main deck.

Outcome:

- Harvus accomplishes little materially.
- He does not get caught.
- He gains familiarity with the Wormwood's restricted geography and confirms that the officer spaces are difficult targets.
- This can quietly seed his later willingness to attempt infiltration on the Man's Promise when Sandara asks him to investigate officer or captain spaces.
