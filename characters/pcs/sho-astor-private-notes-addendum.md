## Newly Locked Character Traits

### Vermin / Gross-Thing Aversion

Sho is **terribly freaked out by bugs, rats, and things she considers gross**.

When directly confronted with them, the contemporaneous private character note says she is likely to **panic, fight, or run**.

This is a real Sho character trait, not a 2026 reconstruction inference.

Use carefully when reconstructing Bonewrack vermin, swarm, insect, or foul-environment scenes. It can explain or color reactions, but must not replace direct log evidence for what Sho actually did in a specific scene.

### Magnimar Goal

Sho **fully intends to find an opportunity to make her way to Magnimar**.

This upgrades Magnimar from a loose backstory / emotional anchor to an explicit personal destination and future objective.

Do not automatically equate the Magnimar goal with finding Agatha Dreamriver there unless another source explicitly connects the two.
