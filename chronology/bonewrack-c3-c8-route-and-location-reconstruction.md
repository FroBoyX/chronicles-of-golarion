## Locked Route Spine

The first expedition follows this broad route:

1. **C3 Abandoned Fishing Village** - landing and tracks inland.
2. **C4 The Mire** - destroyed bridge, pilings, branches, and giant frogs.
3. **First camp between C4 and C5** - hidden alchemist's fire and three-undead night attack.
4. **C5 Whores' Boudoir** - rotting tree-and-tent lair, later burned into the `great tree fire` landmark.
5. **C6 Crab Palm Beach**, called **Coconut Beach** in play - coconuts, giant crab, lone undead figure, and injury-driven recovery nearby.
6. **C7 Fields** - overgrown cornfield, observed but not fully explored.
7. **C8 Castaway's Stockade** - spring, spyglass, vine chokers, Arron Ivy, and the route clue toward Riptide Cove.
8. **D Riptide Cove** - first failed assault and later successful rescue.
9. **C9 Infernus wreck** - sighted offshore but not explored.

## C3 - Landing and Return Point

### Locked

- C3 is the original northern-beach landing site.
- The collapsed huts are the abandoned village used in the player narratives.
- The path from C3 leads directly toward C4.
- Rak and Sho later return to this same village before the Scourge confrontation.

This removes the prior ambiguity between an unnamed landing beach and the abandoned village.

## C4 - The Mire and Broken Bridge

### Locked physical identity

The played broken crossing is C4:

- the river / bog is roughly 70 feet across;
- only bank platforms and pilings remain from the bridge;
- branches overhang the mire;
- Rak attempts to jump the pilings;
- a giant frog catches or attacks him;
- Mirei disables or entrances the frogs long enough for escape.

### Frog survival

The first party does not preserve a confirmed frog kill. Rak later guides Syl's supply party through the same hazard and frogs attack again.

The strongest reconstruction is that one or both original C4 frogs survived the first encounter. Do not require this if later combat records prove replacement or additional frogs.

## First Camp and the C5 Ghouls

### Camp placement

The first camp occurs after the C4 crossing and before the party reaches C5.

This fits:

- Sho's account of crossing the bridge before making camp;
- Mirei's hidden flask placement during that camp;
- the next travel period leading into swamps, insects, and the rotting site;
- the AP route from C4 toward C5.

### Identity of the three undead women

The AP gives C5 exactly three ghoul women who leave their lair at night to hunt along the island paths.

The first camp is attacked at night by exactly three undead women.

The strongest identity match is therefore:

> The first-night attackers were the three C5 ghouls hunting away from the Whores' Boudoir.

This is stronger than treating the night attackers and C5 occupants as unrelated groups.

### Fate remains qualified

The surviving accounts do not clearly establish:

- how many of the three died at the camp;
- whether any escaped back toward C5;
- whether the lair was occupied when the party found it;
- or whether the lone undead later seen at C6 was one of these ghouls.

Do not invent a complete casualty sequence.

## C5 - Whores' Boudoir and the Great Tree Fire

### Locked identity

The foul tree / rotting camp is C5:

- great tree;
- tent-like covering;
- rotting humanoid remains;
- flies and insects;
- lurid decoration;
- disease concern;
- later burning.

The burned C5 site becomes the `great tree fire` landmark used during later route descriptions and the post-Riptide camp.

### Mirei's hidden flask

The hidden alchemist's fire was placed during the earlier camp, before the party reached C5.

The AP places six additional alchemist's-fire flasks inside C5, but there is no table evidence that the party recovered them.

The hidden flask and the later C5 fire must not be treated as the same object or causal event without Mirei / GM confirmation.

### Treasure

No C5 treasure is granted by default. Most of the island was not looted.

## C6 - Crab Palm Beach / Coconut Beach

### Name reconciliation

**Crab Palm Beach** is the AP name.

**Coconut Beach** is the table / archive name used for the same played location.

Treat these as one location rather than two beaches.

### Played changes

- The AP provides three giant crabs in separate palm trees.
- The played scene activates one giant crab prominently.
- That crab drops onto and kills a lone undead figure.
- The party avoids fighting it.
- Sho and Harvus gather coconuts while Rak makes the carrying basket.
- The party takes its injury-driven recovery in the beach / nearby rocky-slope area.

### Lone undead figure

The AP does not place an undead creature at C6.

The lone undead is therefore a campaign-specific addition or movement from another location. An escaped C5 ghoul is plausible, but not confirmed.

## C7 - Fields

### Locked use

- The overgrown field is the cornfield Rak inspects with the spyglass.
- The party does not fully enter or clear it.
- Harvus and Mirei later skirt it after the rescue.
- Rak's escort branch later reaches the same route area before the fork.

The field lies between the eastern beach route and the stockade / southwest route, matching the overview map.

## C8 - Castaway's Stockade

### Locked played elements

The party finds:

- the timber stockade;
- the fresh-water spring;
- vine creatures in the great tree;
- the hanged Arron Ivy inside the lodge;
- the fixed spyglass aimed at Riptide Cove.

Rak's report identifies the vine creatures and hanged inhabitant but does not preserve a completed fight or looting sequence. Do not assume the vine chokers, Ivy, or botfly swarm were destroyed unless direct combat evidence appears.

### Fresh water

C8 is the only confirmed island fresh-water source.

Rak fills his waterskin there and later reports the spring to Scourge.

### First tricorne sighting

The AP spyglass shows two grindylows in D1, one wearing Sandara's tricorne.

Rak's narrative independently says he spotted grindylows from the stronghold wearing items that linked them to the ship attack.

The strongest sequence is now:

1. **First tricorne sighting:** through the fixed C8 spyglass toward D1.
2. **Second sighting:** worn by a grindylow in D3 during the first cave assault.
3. **Third sighting:** hat-wearing grindylow present in D8 during the successful rescue.
4. **Final state:** hat carrier escapes with the Queen; Sandara does not recover the tricorne.

This resolves the earlier stockade-versus-cave first-sighting question.

### Spyglass possession

Rak later calls it his `spiffy new spyglass` and uses it at the route fork to inspect the cornfield.

The strongest reconstruction is that Rak removed the fixed spyglass from C8 and retained it.

This is one of the few specifically supported island acquisitions. It does not imply that the other C8 treasure was taken.

## C9 - Wreck of the Infernus

- The wreck lies offshore southwest of the island.
- Rak, Sho, and Harvus accounts support sighting a wreck from the cliff / open-sea side of Riptide Cove.
- Nobody investigates it during the preserved island sequence.

- Coconuts gathered at C6.
- Fresh water taken from C8.

### Not granted without further evidence

- C5 alchemist's fire, potion, daggers, clothing, perfume, gems, or coin;

- C8 lodge armor, weapons, ring, clothing, spices, or silver goods;
- C9 wreck treasure;
- any creature kill not preserved by table evidence.

## Locked Summary

- C3 is the landing and later return village.
- C4 is the broken bridge / frog mire.
- First camp lies after C4 and before C5.
- The three first-night undead women are best identified as the three C5 hunting ghouls.
- C5 is the burned rotting tree camp and later great-tree landmark.
- C6 Crab Palm Beach and Coconut Beach are the same played location.
- The lone C6 undead is campaign-specific; escaped C5 ghoul remains possible but unproven.
- C7 is the overgrown cornfield.
- C8 contains the spring, vine chokers, Arron Ivy, and fixed spyglass.
- The tricorne is first seen through the C8 spyglass, then in D3 and D8, and leaves with the Queen's hat carrier.
- Rak most likely removed and retained the C8 spyglass.
- C9 is sighted but uninvestigated.
- Most AP treasure remains unlooted.
