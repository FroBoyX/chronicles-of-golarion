## Newly Resolved Sequence

### First Riptide Cove assault

Rak's player narrative establishes from Rak's perspective:

1. The party approached Riptide Cove from the south.
2. They saw a grindylow with Sandara's hat.
3. They identified two water routes, one carrying current inward and one carrying current outward.
4. Stirges attacked while the party climbed down.
5. All four PCs entered through the less-dangerous outflow cave.
6. Sho was ambushed by four grindylows.
7. Sho killed two before going down.
8. Rak killed the other two, pulled Sho to the surface, and handed her to Mirei.
9. Harvus continued farther into the cave.
10. Rak held the entrance because he lacked darkvision and wanted to prevent an attack from behind.
11. Sho and Mirei called for retreat.
12. Mirei placed a light effect on Rak's clothing.
13. Rak killed pursuing grindylows and went deeper to find Harvus.
14. Harvus passed out during the withdrawal.
15. Rak physically carried or dragged Harvus out through the second cave into the open ocean.
16. The whole party regained the shore.

This closes the open questions about four-PC participation, Rak's role in Sho's rescue, and whether Rak physically recovered Harvus.

## Post-Assault Route

The narrative establishes this relative order:

1. The party retrieved Rak's rope and regained the cliff / shore.
2. Rak saw offshore shipwreckage.
3. The group moved back along its explored route.
4. Rak refilled his waterskin at the stronghold spring.
5. The group recovered the coconuts.
6. Rak briefly inspected the unexplored fork and confirmed an overgrown cornfield.
7. The party returned to the burned-tree / `great tree fire` landmark.
8. They camped there for the night.
9. Rak brewed a potion during watch.
10. The party split the following morning.

The post-assault camp and split location should therefore be treated as the burned-tree camp.

Do not place the split at:

- Riptide Cove;
- the cliff overlook;
- Coconut Beach;
- the stockade;
- or the original village.

## Morning Split and Village Return

The narrative establishes:

- Rak and Sho left together for the boat / village.
- Harvus and Mirei left together to return to Riptide Cove.
- Rak and Sho crossed the river / broken-route obstacle with the coconuts.
- They reached the original village together.
- The trip from the split camp to the village was uneventful.
- Man's Promise crew were already ashore searching the village.
- Scourge immediately demanded to know where Harvus and Mirei were.
- Rak reported the food and water discoveries.
- Scourge ordered an unarmed beating.
- Samms struck Rak but appeared reluctant.
- Scourge called the attack off Rak and continued beating Sho.

Later GM clarification establishes that the remaining village-to-ship movement was also uneventful.

## Rak's Report to Scourge

Rak explicitly calls his day-by-day account an `edited version`.

Therefore:

- treat its route skeleton and day count as evidence;
- do not treat every claimed event, omission, camp location, or motive as objective truth;
- do not let the claimed camp outside the stronghold override the GM-locked Coconut Beach recovery;
- recognize that Rak minimizes danger, exaggerates leadership, and conceals the party fracture.

## Date Count

Rak's report divides the expedition into:

- `First day`;
- `Second day`;
- `The third day`;
- `Today`, meaning the morning return to the village.

If the first landing is Arodus 1, the village return occurs on Arodus 4.

The count is especially important because it independently matches Harvus' relative sequence:

- landing sequence / Day 28 = Arodus 1;
- Day 29 = Arodus 2;
- Day 30 = Arodus 3;
- Day 31 = Arodus 4.

The day count appears inside an edited in-character report, so it is not sufficient alone. However, the date itself is not an obvious subject of deception, and the same four-day structure is preserved independently by Harvus.

## Working Date Conclusion

The combined evidence now strongly supports:

- **Arodus 1:** grounding, landing, first camp, ghouls;
- **Arodus 2:** swamp, swarms, burned camp, Coconut Beach recovery;
- **Arodus 3:** stockade / spring / spyglass, first Riptide assault, retreat, burned-tree camp;
- **Arodus 4:** morning split, Rak and Sho return to the village, Harvus and Mirei return to Riptide Cove, village confrontation, rescue, Sho confinement, and Rak's later escorted supply route.

The repeated `Aeodus 5th` label in the final-session log is now best understood as the historical GM date-entry error unless the Sho or Harvus narratives preserve a contradictory additional day.

The Sho and Harvus narratives remain valuable for corroboration, motive, perception, and exact action details. They are no longer required to supply the basic four-day chronology.
