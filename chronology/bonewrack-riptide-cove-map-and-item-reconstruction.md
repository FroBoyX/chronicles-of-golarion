## First Assault Route

### Locked approach

1. Rak, Sho, Mirei, and Harvus approach Riptide Cove from the ridge / island side.
2. Rak provides climbing equipment.
3. The party descends the D1 cliffs.
4. Four stirges attack from the D1 nest.
5. The party enters through **D3, the Fronds**, from D1.

D3 is the played entrance. D2, the Neck, is the long flooded passage connecting D1 to the open sea and was not the first-entry route.

### Tide and current

- Rak remembered water flowing outward.
- Sho remembered water flowing inward.
- Current GM memory is that the tide or current was pushing swimmers toward D2, the Neck, while the party entered through D3.

This resolves the route without forcing a single hydrological label. Lock `D1 -> D3`. Leave exact inflow / outflow terminology open unless later table evidence preserves the tide phase and direction.

## First Assault Combat State

### D1 stirges

- The AP activates four stirges at one time.
- The VTT state shows four active stirges.
- Harvus remembers one attaching to him before he submerged.

This is a strong AP-map-to-play match.

### D3 grindylows

Current GM memory places five recorded D3 grindylow deaths:

- two near the entrance;
- one in the middle;
- two near the far end.

Player narratives establish that:

- Sho killed two rear attackers before falling;
- Rak killed the remaining two attackers around Sho and recovered her;
- Harvus killed additional grindylows deeper in the chamber / route.

The most likely reconciliation is that Sho and Rak account for four of the five recorded D3 deaths, while Harvus accounts for at least one additional death. Exact token-to-killer attribution remains qualified unless initiative records survive.

Harvus estimated roughly eleven grindylows across the encounter. Do not reduce that memory to the AP's eight-D3 baseline. Campaign-adjusted numbers, movement from connected tunnels, repeated exchanges, or poor visibility may explain the difference.

### D4 and D7

- The party avoided D4.
- No grindylow casualties are recorded in D7.
- Do not infer D7 deaths or reinforcements merely because D7 contains grindylows in the AP.

### Hat carrier

1. Sandara's tricorne was in grindylow possession in D3.
2. A grindylow wore it during the first assault.
3. Harvus intended to kill that creature last.
4. The creature escaped deeper.
5. It later appeared in or around D8.
6. Current GM belief is that the hat carrier escaped with the Brinebrood Queen.

The hat carrier's exact route is unresolved, but its current fate is no longer open for ordinary reconstruction: it escaped with the Queen unless later play brings it back.

### Harvus's deep push and collapse

Harvus pushed deeper, withdrew into a side tunnel, drank a potion, killed pursuing grindylows, returned toward Rak, then lost consciousness during the retreat.

Current GM memory narrows the collapse to **D3 or D7**. Exact square remains unresolved. Do not place the collapse at D8 or after the party had already regained the cliff.

The exact D3-to-D8 route remains unresolved. Do not infer it from the orange VTT ruler without knowing the selected actor and turn.

## D8 Cauldron State

### Campaign occupants

- Brinebrood Queen.
- The Whale.
- Two chained lacedons at the bottom.
- Hat-wearing grindylow.
- Devilfish moved into D8 by the GM.
- Dead octopus in the pool.
- Sandara, Tilly, and campaign-added captive Goyle.

The AP baseline has two captives. Goyle is a GM-added third captive. The reason for adding him is no longer remembered, but his presence is table canon.

### Captive suspension and drop

The captives were hanging from the ceiling, bound and weighted. That setup also exists in the AP and was not necessarily a campaign addition.

In play, Harvus scouted and returned to Mirei before the encounter fully opened. During the attack, the captives were knocked or dropped into the Cauldron.

Do not use the AP's immediate trigger to erase the played scouting and healing beat.

### Devilfish

The GM moved the D5 devilfish into D8. The reason is no longer remembered.

Its role in the final encounter is locked:

- it was present in the Cauldron;
- it chose the dead Whale as food;
- the Whale's corpse diverted it from Harvus and the captives;
- it survived the immediate rescue sequence unless later evidence says otherwise.

### Octopus

The VTT / encounter state contains a dead octopus in the Cauldron.

The GM does not remember adding a separate squid. The AP Queen summons an octopus and explicitly never summons a squid. Harvus's retrospective `small squid monster` is therefore best normalized as the **Queen-summoned octopus**, which Harvus killed.

## Whale Encounter Order

### GM-locked sequence

1. Harvus begins descending into D8, starting the encounter.
2. The Whale charges upward from below.
3. Harvus braces Rak's spear against the charge.
4. Harvus scores an actual critical hit.
5. The spear deals triple damage with a strong damage roll, killing the Whale.
6. Harvus continues downward using the Whale's corpse as cover.
7. He reaches and frees Tilly, Sandara, and Goyle.
8. He begins the return ascent with the captives.
9. The devilfish feeds on the Whale's corpse.
10. The remaining grindylows flee.

The Whale dies **before** the captives are freed.

Harvus's retrospective sequence placing the Whale attack during the captive ascent is superseded as objective combat order. It remains preserved as player-authored memory.

### AP compatibility

The AP gives the Whale bite, grab, swallow whole, and confinement to D8. Those mechanics support the charge and brace interaction, but the actual critical hit and encounter order come from GM truth and table play.
