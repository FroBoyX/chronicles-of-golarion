## First Assault Route

### Locked approach

1. Rak, Sho, Mirei, and Harvus approach Riptide Cove from the south / ridge side.
2. Rak provides climbing equipment.
3. The party descends the D1 cliffs.
4. Stirges attack from the D1 nest.
5. The party enters the lair through **D3, the Fronds**, from D1.

### Why D3 is the first entrance

- D3 is the lair entrance directly accessible from D1.
- The AP places eight defending grindylows and Sandara's tricorne in D3.
- The party immediately encounters grindylows, including the hat-wearing creature.
- D2 instead connects D1 to the open sea and contains ocean-side sentries farther along the flooded neck.

The first assault therefore did not begin by entering through D2 from the sea.

### Current-direction conflict

Rak remembers taking the cave where water flowed outward. Sho remembers the selected cave as having water flowing inward.

This does not indicate different physical entrances. Both accounts describe the D1-to-D3 choice, but they may describe current relative to D1, the tunnel interior, the open sea, the direction faced, or the tide at that moment.

The AP map and text do not preserve the tide phase or instantaneous current direction during the played scene. Lock **D3 entry**, but leave `inflow` / `outflow` terminology unresolved.

## First Assault Combat State

### D1 stirges

- The AP activates four stirges at one time.
- The preserved VTT map shows four active stirge tokens at D1.
- Harvus remembers one attaching to him before he submerged.

This is a strong map-to-play match.

### D3 grindylows

- The AP places eight grindylows in D3.
- Sho guards the rear and is ambushed by four.
- Sho kills two before falling.
- Rak kills the remaining two and recovers Sho.
- Harvus fights deeper in the chamber and estimates roughly eleven grindylows overall.

The exact eleven-count cannot be derived from the AP's D3 population alone. Possibilities include reinforcements from another chamber, campaign-adjusted creature counts, Harvus counting opponents across more than one exchange, or retrospective overcount under poor visibility.

Do not reduce Harvus's memory to eight merely because the AP baseline says eight.

### Hat carrier

The strongest reconstruction is:

1. Sandara's tricorne entered D3 as captive equipment under the AP scaffold.
2. A D3 grindylow wore it during the first assault.
3. Harvus deliberately spared it for last, but it fled deeper.
4. Harvus later saw the same or another hat-wearing grindylow in D8 during the successful rescue.

This links D3 to D8 without proving the creature's exact route or final fate.

### Harvus's deep push and collapse

Harvus advances beyond Sho and Rak's rear fight, withdraws into a side tunnel, drinks a potion, kills pursuing grindylows, and returns toward the entrance.

The accounts place his collapse after he returns toward Rak, during the withdrawal, and before the party completes the D2 swim to the open sea.

The best current placement is near the D3 / D1-side retreat transition, not at the Cauldron and not after the party had already reached the cliff. Exact square remains unresolved.

## First Assault Retreat Route

### Locked retreat

1. Retreat from D3 into D1.
2. Enter **D2, the Neck** from D1.
3. Swim through the long flooded passage.
4. Exit to the open sea.
5. Regain the cliff / shore and recover the rope.

This matches Rak's statement that he carried Harvus through the other cave into the open ocean, Sho's account of braving the current through the other tunnel, the map's only direct D1-to-open-sea connection, and the AP description of D2 as a long, always-flooded, churning neck.

## Second Assault Route

### Locked sea-side entry

1. Harvus and Mirei approach from the sea side rather than descending into D1 from the island cliffs.
2. They enter through D2's ocean entrance.
3. Four grindylow sentries oppose them.
4. Harvus kills all four.
5. They pass through D2 into D1 and return to D3.

The four sentries are an exact match between Harvus's account, the AP D2 population, and the preserved VTT setup.

### D3 as the probable junction

Harvus describes reaching a chamber with three possible exits and hearing creatures through only one route.

D3 is the strongest candidate because it lies directly after D1 on the return route, the first assault had already broken its defenders, it functions as a major junction into the inner cave system, and the previously occupied tunnels now seem eerily empty.

This remains a strong inference rather than an exact recovered room label.

### Inner route to D8

The exact D3-to-D8 path is not preserved well enough to lock whether Harvus and Mirei passed through D7, D5, or another connecting tunnel sequence.

Do not infer the route from the orange VTT ruler. The selected actor is unknown.

## D8 Cauldron Reconstruction

### Occupants supported by play and scaffold

- Brinebrood Queen.
- The Whale.
- Two chained aquatic ghouls / lacedons at the bottom.
- Hat-wearing grindylow surviving from or associated with the first assault.
- Devilfish moving into or already present in the Cauldron.
- Sandara, Tilly, and campaign-added captive Goyle.

The AP baseline has only two captives. Goyle is a campaign-specific addition and remains table canon.

### Detection and captive drop

The AP drops captives immediately when intruders enter D8.

In play:

1. Invisible Harvus scouts D8.
2. He returns to Mirei.
3. Mirei heals him.
4. Noise or spellcasting alerts the lair.
5. The captives are dropped into the water.

The played delay overrides the AP's immediate trigger.

### Chained ghouls

Harvus's pair of shackled ghouls at the bottom directly matches the two D8 lacedons.

### Devilfish

Harvus sees a large toothed tentacled creature while descending and later sees it release a concealment-like burst and feed on the Whale's corpse.

This strongly identifies the creature as the D5 devilfish, using or resembling its AP concealment ability after moving into D8.

Its survival is table-specific. The AP does not require it to die.

### Small squid creature

Harvus calls the creature attacking Tilly a small squid monster.

The AP says the Queen uses her summoning wand for an **octopus** and explicitly never summons a squid.

The best identification is therefore:

> Queen-summoned octopus, described by Harvus as a squid-like creature.

Harvus kills it during the ascent.

## Whale Sequence

### Corrected sequence

The strongest sequence is Harvus's detailed mechanical account:

1. Harvus enters D8 invisibly.
2. The Whale passes above him during the descent and narrowly misses him.
3. Harvus reaches the captives at the bottom.
4. He frees Tilly, Sandara, and Goyle.
5. He begins ascending with Sandara.
6. The Whale lunges for Sandara and Harvus.
7. Harvus braces Rak's spear with a hero point and rages.
8. The Whale attempts to swallow them.
9. Harvus critically wounds / kills it while shielding Sandara from its bites.
10. The devilfish feeds on the corpse.

### AP compatibility

The AP gives the Whale bite, grab, swallow whole, confinement to D8, and death-fight morale.

Those mechanics closely match Harvus's remembered ascent fight. The AP does **not** require the Whale to die before the captives are freed.

Therefore the earlier reconstruction that placed the Whale's death in the opening exchange before captive release is superseded.

## Brinebrood Queen

### Outcome

- Harvus sees an angry female grindylow before the rescue.
- GM reconstruction locks the Brinebrood Queen as surviving and fleeing after the Whale dies.
- The AP gives the Queen the same morale trigger: she flees when the Whale is slain.

This is a strong AP-supported GM outcome.

### Knowledge boundary

Harvus does not directly narrate witnessing the escape.

Do not automatically give Harvus, Mirei, or the rescued captives visual knowledge of the Queen's exact route or survival unless another source establishes it.

The orange VTT movement ruler cannot identify her escape path without actor / turn context.

## Item State

### Rak's +1 returning spear

**Last confirmed event:** Harvus loses the spear while answering Mirei's message and fails to catch it with his feet.

Current reconstruction:

- The spear falls into D8.
- It is presumed lost in the Cauldron unless later recovered.
- Its returning property does not by itself undo the loss because Harvus dropped it rather than throwing it as a returning attack.

Do not conflate Rak's spear with the Queen's separate AP returning harpoon. Rak lent his spear before the Queen was encountered.

### Harvus's flail

**Last confirmed event:** Harvus gives the flail to Tilly after freeing her.

Harvus later explicitly believes Tilly lost his weapon during the ascent.

Current reconstruction:

- The flail is presumed lost in D8.
- Tilly should not appear at the fork carrying it unless later inventory or direct evidence contradicts Harvus's account.

### Sandara's tricorne

AP baseline places the tricorne in D3.

Campaign sequence moves it onto a grindylow: worn in D3 during the first assault, carrier flees, and the hat is seen again in D8 during the second assault.

No surviving account records recovery.

Current reconstruction:

- Sandara does **not** have confirmed possession at the fork.
- The tricorne remains unaccounted for in D8 or with the hat carrier's final state.
- Do not import the AP post-rescue gift scene.

### Sandara's Besmara holy symbol

Campaign evidence differs from the AP.

- Rak gives the symbol to Harvus before the island expedition.
- It is not part of Sandara's D3 / D8 captive equipment.
- No surviving rescue account records Harvus returning it.

Current reconstruction:

- Harvus remains the last confirmed holder.
- A later transfer back to Sandara is possible but not yet evidenced.

### Queen's returning harpoon

The AP Queen carries a separate returning harpoon.

Because the Queen survives and flees under current canon, presume the harpoon leaves with her unless disarm, drop, or recovery evidence appears. Do not add it to PC treasure.

### Other AP treasure and captive gear

No surviving account records a deliberate post-fight search or treasure collection.

Do not grant D4, D5, D7, or D8 treasure, silver ingots, captive equipment scattered on the D8 floor, Queen gear, or AP story-reward items without table evidence.

Sandara and Tilly may therefore remain missing some or most equipment after rescue.
