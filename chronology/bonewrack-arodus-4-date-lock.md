## Document Status

- Type: Canon chronology ruling
- Maturity: Locked
- Scope: Objective calendar date for the final Bonewrack split-session branches
- Supersedes: The working conclusion in `chronology/bonewrack-final-session-date-audit.md`
- Historical labels preserved: `Aeodus 5th` and `Arodus 5th, 4701` remain quoted source metadata only

## Ruling

The final Bonewrack split-session branches occurred on **Arodus 4, 4712 AR**.

The live-session labels `Aeodus 5th` and the Harvus heading `Arodus 5th, 4701 (Day 31 pt. 2)` are historical date-entry errors. Preserve them in raw evidence and direct quotations, but do not use them as objective campaign chronology.

All within-day branch times remain usable, subject to the already identified isolated AM/PM errors and the GM's deliberate out-of-order scene reveals.

## Evidence Basis

### 1. Grounding anchor

- The Man's Promise grounds and the island party departs on Arodus 1.

### 2. Three-rest structure

The island expedition contains three recovery periods:

1. first camp after landing, interrupted by the ghoul attack;
2. Coconut Beach-area recovery after the swamp and swarm damage;
3. burned-tree camp after the failed Riptide Cove assault.

There is no fourth rest and no separate stockade rest.

### 3. Harvus day sequence

Harvus preserves:

- Day 28: landing, frogs, first camp, ghouls;
- Day 29: swamp, swarms, burned site, beach, camp;
- Day 30: first Riptide assault, retreat, burned-tree camp;
- Day 31: successful return, rescue, aftermath, and movement toward the fork.

Harvus's `Day 31 pt. 2` directly continues the Day 31 rescue and explicitly calls the first rescue attempt `yesterday` and the successful return `today`.

The same heading gives the campaign year as `4701`, proving that its metadata is not reliable even though the event sequence is.

### 4. Sho day sequence

Sho independently preserves:

- Day 28;
- Day 29;
- a missing-heading Day 30 cave fragment;
- Day 31 morning split and village return.

### 5. Rak day sequence

Rak's edited report to Scourge divides the expedition into:

- first day;
- second day;
- third day;
- `today`, when Rak and Sho return to the village.

Although the report is deliberately edited, the four-day count independently matches Harvus and Sho and is not an obvious subject of deception.

## Locked Date Mapping

### Arodus 1

- Man's Promise grounds.
- Rak, Sho, Mirei, and Harvus land.
- Broken bridge / frog encounter.
- First camp.
- Mirei hides the alchemist's fire.
- Three undead women / ghouls attack during the night.

### Arodus 2

- Jungle and swamp travel.
- Swarm attacks and heavy injury.
- Sho becomes separated briefly in the swamp and rejoins near the burning site.
- Foul tree / rotting camp burns.
- Coconut Beach discovered.
- Giant crab kills the lone undead figure.
- Coconuts gathered.
- Party takes its injury-driven recovery in the Coconut Beach / nearby rocky-slope area.

### Arodus 3

- Party continues to the stockade, spring, spyglass, and cove route.
- First Riptide Cove assault.
- Sho goes down and Rak rescues her.
- Harvus pushes deeper, is critically wounded, and collapses during retreat.
- Rak carries Harvus out.
- Party retraces the route, checks the cornfield fork, and camps at the still-burning tree landmark.

### Arodus 4

- Party splits in the morning at the burned-tree camp.
- Harvus and Mirei return to Riptide Cove.
- Rak and Sho return to the original village and then continue uneventfully to the Man's Promise.
- Scourge orders their beating at the village.
- Harvus and Mirei conduct the successful rescue.
- Goyle surfaces and reports Plugg's betrayal.
- Mirei confesses Plugg's order to kill the other PCs.
- Rescue group leaves the cove, skirts the cornfield, and approaches the fork.
- Sho's confinement branch and Rak's hostile escort branch proceed using the preserved within-day times.
