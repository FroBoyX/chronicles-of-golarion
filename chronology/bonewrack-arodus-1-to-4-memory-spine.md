## Calendar Lock

The island chronology is:

- **Erastus 31:** Storm and initial grindylow abductions.
- **Arodus 1:** Grounding, first landing, broken bridge/frogs, first camp, hidden flask, ghoul attack.
- **Arodus 2:** Swamp and swarms, burning site, Coconut Beach, injury-forced recovery.
- **Arodus 3:** Stockade/spring/spyglass, first Riptide assault, retreat, burned-tree camp.
- **Arodus 4:** Morning split, Rak/Sho village return, Harvus/Mirei successful rescue, Scourge beating, Sho confinement, Rak escort branch, rescue group movement toward the fork.

The repeated historical `Aeodus 5th` label is a one-day-high GM entry error. Harvus's `Arodus 5th, 4701 (Day 31 pt. 2)` heading also gives the wrong campaign year and directly continues Day 31, proving the metadata unreliable while preserving the event sequence.

## Neutral Chronology Spine

### 0. Erastus 31 — Storm and Initial Abductions

**Confirmed**

- A severe storm drives the Man's Promise toward Bonewrack.
- Sandara Quinn and Tilly Brackett are taken by grindylows during the storm.
- Goyle Onorach is not one of the initial abductees.
- Harvus works in the rigging.
- Sho and Rak fight grindylows on deck.
- Mirei falls from the rigging and Sho cushions or catches her.
- Water pours into the starboard side below deck while Kroop works on the damaged galley.

**Direct dialogue consequences**

- Harvus asks Sho what happened on deck.
- Sho says she and Rak fought grindylows and that they seemed unusually interested in Rak.
- Harvus and Sho go below to help with the flooding.

### 1. Arodus 1 — Grounding and Supply Pressure

**Confirmed**

- The Man's Promise grounds on Bonewrack's reef.
- The water barrels are ruptured.
- Plugg estimates repair will take at least a day and a half and says the ship will sail at a later tide.
- Food and fresh water must be found ashore.
- Sandara and Tilly remain missing.

**Mirei's private branch**

- Syl reports Mirei's earlier contact with Sho to Plugg.
- Plugg physically confronts Mirei and gives her a whispered loyalty task.
- Mirei later admits that the task was to ensure the expedition became one-way for Rak, Sho, and Harvus.
- The exact whispered wording remains unrecovered.

**Island party**

- Rak Wraithraiser
- Sho Astor
- Mirei
- Harvus G'ron

### 2. Arodus 1 — Landing and Broken Bridge

**Confirmed across chat, Sho, and Harvus**

- The four PCs land at the abandoned village / original beach.
- They follow signs or tracks inland.
- They reach a destroyed bridge across a swampy river.
- Most use branches, stumps, or climbing routes.
- Rak attempts to jump the remaining bridge stumps in a `PARKOUR` maneuver.
- A large frog creature catches or attacks Rak.
- Mirei uses magic to entrance or disable the frogs long enough for Rak to escape.

**Closed question**

- The frog encounter occurs before the first camp.

### 3. Arodus 1 — First Camp, Hidden Flask, and Undead Attack

**Confirmed**

- The party finds a clearing and makes a fire-lit camp with watches.
- Sho quickly falls asleep.
- Mirei waits until the others appear asleep, sneaks away, and places an alchemist's fire in a tree along the path without leaving obvious signs.
- During the night, three undead women attack.
- Harvus places the attack on third watch.
- At least one male PC may have been bitten, but Sho observes no immediate consequence.
- The party survives and completes enough rest to continue.

**Unresolved**

- Mirei's intended purpose for the hidden flask.
- Whether that flask later caused or contributed to the burning site.

### 4. Arodus 2 — Swamp and Swarms

**Confirmed across chat and Sho**

- The party moves through dense jungle into swamp or marsh.
- Rak distributes alchemist's fire when the buzzing grows louder.
- Swarms of mosquitoes, botflies, or similar insects attack.
- Sho and Harvus suffer serious damage.
- Sho panics and dives into the swamp, initially swimming back along the route.
- Sho later reverses course and rejoins the others after seeing smoke.
- The group uses fire and alchemy against the swarms and/or contaminated site.
- Sho gives Harvus one cure-light-wounds potion and drinks another herself.

**Harvus HP correction**

- The raw macro's `-14 / dying / +14 / -4` sequence is a corrected input error, not proof Harvus entered dying in that exact swarm exchange.
- Harvus's broader memory that the swamp/insect sequence nearly killed him remains valid player-perspective evidence.

### 5. Arodus 2 — Burning Site and Coconut Beach

**Confirmed**

- The party reaches a foul tree or rotting camp with flies, dead humanoids, and lurid markings.
- The site burns and becomes the later `great tree fire` landmark.
- Sho is briefly separated and rejoins the group near the fire.
- The party reaches Coconut Beach.
- The lone humanoid there is undead.
- A giant crab drops from a palm tree and kills the undead figure instantly.
- The party avoids fighting the crab.
- Sho and Harvus climb palms and gather coconuts.
- Harvus cuts leaves while Rak constructs a carrying basket.

### 6. Arodus 2 — Coconut Beach Recovery

**GM lock with Sho corroboration**

- Accumulated swarm damage forces a full recovery stop in the Coconut Beach area.
- Sho describes the camp on rocky slopes after leaving the immediate palms and returning toward the road.
- Treat this as the Coconut Beach-area recovery, not a separate additional camp.
- The stop is injury-driven rather than caused solely by ordinary nightfall.

### 7. Arodus 3 — Stockade, Spring, Spyglass, and Riptide Route

**Confirmed / strongly supported**

- After recovering, the party continues uphill.
- They reach the stockade / stronghold.
- A fresh-water spring runs from or near it.
- The stronghold includes hostile vine creatures and a hanged inhabitant in Rak's report.
- Rak obtains or uses a spyglass.
- Sandara's hat is seen on a grindylow, either from the stockade or at the cove.
- The party identifies Riptide Cove as the likely location of the captives.

**No separate rest**

- The stockade/spring/spyglass sequence and the first Riptide assault occur in the same active day.

### 8. Arodus 3 — First Riptide Cove Assault

**Confirmed across Rak, Sho, and Harvus**

- The party approaches from the south through jungle and ridge slopes.
- The cove descent is approximately 40–60 feet.
- Rak supplies climbing equipment and a spear.
- Stirges attack during the descent.
- All four PCs enter the cave system.
- Harvus calls for Sandara.
- Harvus estimates roughly eleven grindylows.
- Sho and Rak have little or no useful vision beyond the sunlight.
- Sho guards the rear.
- Four grindylows ambush from behind.
- Sho kills two and is dropped by the other two.
- Rak kills the remaining two, pulls Sho to the surface, and hands her to Mirei.
- Mirei revives Sho with a potion.
- Harvus continues deeper.
- Rak initially guards the entrance because he lacks darkvision and wants to prevent another rear attack.
- Sho and Mirei call for retreat.
- Mirei casts light on Rak's trousers.
- Pursuing grindylows become visible and are killed.
- Rak goes deeper to find Harvus.

### 9. Arodus 3 — Harvus's Deep Push and Retreat

**Harvus-perspective sequence**

- Harvus enters rage using a hero point.
- He kills several grindylows.
- The grindylow carrying Sandara's hat flees deeper.
- Harvus takes multiple spear hits, including a critical.
- He withdraws into a side tunnel and drinks a potion.
- Two grindylows follow.
- Harvus kills one and violently kills the creature that had critically struck him.
- Harvus retreats toward Rak and asks to be carried.
- His rage ends and he falls unconscious.
- Rak physically carries or drags Harvus out through the second cave into the open ocean.

**Cave-current conflict**

- Rak remembers first entering through an outflow cave.
- Sho remembers first entering through an inflow cave.
- The map must resolve the current terminology.

### 10. Arodus 3 — Rope Recovery and Burned-Tree Camp

**Confirmed across all three narratives**

- The party regains the shore.
- Sho and Rak retrieve the rope while Mirei guards Harvus.
- Rak carries Harvus during the final climb.
- Harvus later wakes overlooking the ocean and acknowledges his debt to Rak.
- The group retraces its route past the stockade and spring.
- They recover the coconuts.
- Rak uses the spyglass at the fork and confirms an overgrown cornfield.
- The party returns to the still-burning tree / `great tree fire` landmark.
- Rak brews a potion during watch.
- They camp there for the night.

**Locked split location**

- The party splits the following morning from the burned-tree camp.

### 11. Arodus 4 — Morning Split

**Confirmed**

- Harvus declares he will return for Sandara whether or not the others accompany him.
- Mirei volunteers to go with him.
- Rak gives Harvus a potion and later gives Sho another at her request.
- Rak and Sho return because someone must report food and water and prevent the Man's Promise from leaving the others behind.
- Harvus and Mirei head back to Riptide Cove.
- Rak and Sho head toward the original village.

### 12. Arodus 4 — Rak and Sho Return to the Village

**Confirmed**

- Rak throws most of the coconuts across the destroyed bridge / river.
- Rak and Sho cross, recover the coconuts, and continue.
- They reach the original village together in record time or without incident.
- Man's Promise crew are already ashore searching the village.
- Scourge demands to know where Harvus and Mirei are.
- Rak reports the food and water discoveries and says the others are finishing the grindylow nest.
- Scourge orders the crew to beat Rak and Sho for disobedience.
- No one initially draws weapons.
- Samms strikes Rak but appears reluctant.
- Syl strikes Sho first.
- Ebony attempts to restrain Sho.
- Scourge whips Sho.
- Ebony silently warns Sho not to attack Scourge.
- Sho kneels and is beaten unconscious.

**Afterward**

- Travel from the village to the Man's Promise is uneventful.
- Sho's confinement/hot-box branch follows.
- Rak is questioned and later sent back across Bonewrack with Syl, Jape, Shivikah, and Fipps.

### 13. Goyle's Later Capture

**GM lock**

- Goyle is captured in a separate later grindylow attack while the PCs are away.
- Plugg uses the Tidal Cutlass while Goyle grapples or fights a grindylow.
- Goyle and the creature are washed overboard.
- Goyle is taken to Riptide Cove.

**Placement**

- Goyle says this happened about a day after the PCs left.
- Exact placement within Arodus 2–3 remains approximate, but it occurs before the successful rescue.

### 14. Arodus 4 — Harvus and Mirei Return to Riptide Cove

**Confirmed from Harvus**

- Harvus and Mirei circumvent the ridge and approach from the sea entrance.
- Four grindylow sentries guard the entrance.
- Harvus kills all four in sequence.
- Harvus and Mirei enter stealthily.
- They reach a chamber with three exits and hear creatures through one route.
- Mirei gives Harvus a potion of invisibility.
- Harvus scouts the flooded central chamber.
- He sees the hat-wearing grindylow, an angry female grindylow, the Whale, Sandara, Tilly, and Goyle.
- Harvus returns to Mirei for cure light wounds.
- The lair detects them and the captives are dropped or sunk into the water.

### 15. Arodus 4 — Cauldron Rescue

**Harvus-perspective sequence**

- Harvus enters the water invisibly.
- The Whale passes above and narrowly misses him at first.
- Harvus passes a devilfish.
- Shackled ghouls guard the bottom.
- Harvus cuts Tilly free and gives her his flail.
- He cuts Sandara free and puts her on his shoulders.
- He cuts Goyle free.
- During the ascent, the Whale lunges for Sandara.
- Harvus uses a hero point to brace Rak's spear.
- He rages and critically kills the Whale while shielding Sandara from its bites.
- The devilfish begins feeding on the Whale's corpse.
- Harvus kills a smaller squid-like creature attacking Tilly.
- Mirei asks through message whether Harvus is alive.
- Harvus gurgles in reply and loses Rak's spear.
- Mirei helps bring Sandara and Tilly above water.
- Goyle is initially missing from Harvus's immediate view.

**Whale-sequence conflict**

- Earlier campaign reconstruction placed the Whale's death in the opening exchange before the captives were freed.
- Harvus's detailed player narrative places it during the ascent after the captives were cut free.
- Preserve this conflict for map/initiative review.

**Queen status**

- Harvus sees an angry female grindylow.
- He does not directly describe her retreat.
- The Brinebrood Queen's flight remains GM-locked campaign outcome pending direct combat-state confirmation.

### 16. Arodus 4 — Immediate Rescue Aftermath

**Confirmed**

- Sandara coughs water and survives.
- Tilly reports that Sandara was beaten by the grindylows.
- Tilly collapses from her own wounds.
- Goyle surfaces alive with tentacle injury.
- Goyle reports Plugg's betrayal and later capture.
- Mirei confesses that Plugg tasked her with killing Rak, Sho, and Harvus.
- Harvus feels anger but accepts that Mirei has just preserved his life.
- The group decides to leave immediately.

### 17. Arodus 4 — Movement Toward the Fork

**Confirmed from Harvus**

- Harvus carries Sandara.
- Goyle supports or carries Tilly.
- The group leaves the caves and reaches shore near the cornfield.
- They rest briefly.
- They avoid entering the cornfield.
- They continue toward the rendezvous route.
- The source ends near the fork when faint voices are heard ahead.

Stop here. Do not restage the fork encounter in this spine.
