# Bonewrack Arodus 1-4 Memory Spine

## Document Status

- Type: GM-side neutral chronology / memory spine
- Maturity: Working
- Scope: The grounding of the Man's Promise, first Bonewrack landing, island exploration and Riptide Cove events during Arodus 1-4, and the transition into the preserved Arodus 5 branches
- Source control layer: `source-spines/bonewrack-island-arodus-1-4.md`
- Player-memory routing: `questions/bonewrack-island-memory-batches.md`
- Player-facing status: Not player-facing by default
- Canon status: Confirmed relative sequence with unresolved internal dating and location order
- Date guardrail: Only Arodus 1 is securely assigned within the island sequence. Do not convert Harvus' relative `Day 29`, `Day 30`, or `Day 31` note labels directly into Arodus 2, 3, or 4.
- Arodus 5 boundary: Stop after the successful rescue and the separation of the surviving branches. Do not redo the fork encounter, Rak's later Syl-led supply run, or Sho's Kahina conversation.

## Evidence Labels

- **Confirmed table event:** Recovered chat, mechanical log, or sanitized session-log extraction.
- **GM-locked event:** Current GM correction or campaign truth.
- **Campaign reconstruction:** Existing archive synthesis supported by multiple sources but lacking complete direct logs.
- **Player note:** Contemporaneous or near-contemporaneous character notes. Strong for that character's relative sequence and perception, not automatic global truth.
- **Player memory:** Later Character Discovery answer. Strong for interiority and remembered interpretation, not automatic global truth.
- **AP scaffold:** Published encounter geography, pressure, or structure. Context only.
- **Unresolved:** Do not silently choose a placement or outcome.

## Chronology Guardrails

The surviving evidence supports a coherent order of major events but not a complete calendar for Arodus 2-4.

Secure anchors:

- **Erastus 31, 4712 AR:** Storm, initial grindylow abductions, and open fight aboard the Man's Promise.
- **Arodus 1, 4712 AR:** Reef grounding, ship assessment, supply pressure, first island departure, first camp, and nighttime ghoul attack.
- **Arodus 1-4:** At least three days of island activity, including exploration, attrition, the first failed Riptide Cove assault, retreat, party split, Goyle's later capture, and the successful return.
- **Immediately before the Arodus 5 rescue-side scene:** Harvus and Mirei have brought Sandara and Tilly above water; Goyle survives and surfaces with them.

## Neutral Memory / Chronology Spine

### 0. Storm and Initial Abductions

**Calendar anchor:** Erastus 31, 4712 AR.

**Confirmed / locked**

- A large storm drives the Man's Promise southwest and forces all hands into emergency ship work.
- Grindylows take **Sandara Quinn** and **Tilly Brackett** during the storm before attacking openly.
- Goyle Onórach is not one of the initial abductees.
- Harvus is in the rigging during part of the open fight.
- Sho and Rak fight grindylows on deck.
- Rak is badly wounded, dropping as low as 2 of 20 hit points before receiving healing.
- Mirei falls from the rigging, and Sho moves beneath her to cushion or catch the fall.
- The galley and starboard side suffer damage and water enters below decks.

**Player-note detail**

- Harvus remembers later going below to bail water and directing anger toward Slippery Syl.

**Unresolved**

- Whether Harvus moves from the rigging to bailing after the preserved fight fragment or whether the note compresses distinct storm phases.
- When the crew realizes Sandara and Tilly are missing.
- Which NPCs are present during the open deck fight.

### 1. Grounding, Ship Damage, and Supply Pressure

**Calendar anchor:** Arodus 1, 4712 AR.

**Confirmed / locked**

- The Man's Promise is blown onto a reef beside Bonewrack Isle.
- The ship's fresh water is gone or unusable.
- The ship is damaged but not immediately lost.
- Repairs are expected to require roughly a day and a half to two days.
- Food and fresh water must be found ashore.
- Tilly and Sandara remain missing.

**Player-note detail**

- Harvus remembers Plugg calling roll, Sandara being missing, and searching the deck with Rak without finding her.
- Rak gives Harvus Sandara's holy symbol before the island search. Harvus later reads this as trust; Rak's player remembers it as a practical choice.

**AP scaffold only**

- The published version places the principal hole in the starboard main hold near the water barrel and uses Sandara's dropped holy symbol as a clue. Do not import those exact details where campaign evidence differs.

### 2. Plugg's Supply Assignment and Mirei's Hidden Task

**Relative placement:** After damage assessment and before the first landing.

**Confirmed table events**

- Syl reports that Mirei went off mission during her crew-gauging assignment by speaking with Sho or the monk.
- Plugg physically confronts Mirei after the storm.
- Plugg gives Mirei a new whispered loyalty test. The exact words are not preserved.
- Plugg orders Rak to take **Sho, Mirei, and Harvus** ashore by rowboat to find food and water.
- Mirei is included because Plugg intends the mission to test her loyalty.

**Character-specific knowledge**

- Only Mirei knows the exact words she heard and what she believed Plugg expected at that moment.
- Her later confession establishes the task's outer shape: the expedition was intended to be one-way for most of the others, and she was expected to kill or betray them.

**Unresolved**

- Exact whispered wording.
- Whether Plugg named specific victims, a method, or a time.

### 3. Initial Island Landing

**Calendar anchor:** Late Arodus 1.

**Party composition**

- Rak Wraithraiser
- Sho Astor
- Mirei
- Harvus G'ron

**Confirmed / strongly supported**

- The four PCs reach Bonewrack by rowboat to search for supplies.
- They find human and nonhuman or monstrous tracks leading inland toward marsh or jungle terrain.
- Sho urges caution and wants to gather supplies and leave quickly.
- The current island reconstruction treats the abandoned fishing village as the original landing area, but the surviving landing evidence does not independently name the village.

**Unresolved**

- Exact landing beach.
- Whether the abandoned fishing village is searched immediately, crossed without investigation, or identified later.

### 4. Jungle, Mire, Broken Bridge, and Frog Threat

**Relative placement:** Early in the first island push.

**Player-note and retrospective support**

- The party crosses bog or mire terrain.
- Rak is attacked by frog-like creatures.
- The route includes a broken bridge or broken crossing that Rak later remembers well enough to guide Syl's Arodus 5 supply party.

**Unresolved**

### 5. First Rest, Hidden Flask, and Camp

**Relative placement:** After the early trail and crossing pressure.

**Confirmed table events**

**Unresolved**

### 9. Beach, Coconut Route, Fields, Stockade, Spring, and Spyglass

**Relative placement:** Between the burned camp and the first Riptide Cove assault. Internal order remains unresolved.

**Supported discoveries**

- Harvus' note records reaching a beach, seeing a lone humanoid, and later camping.
- Before Arodus 5, Rak knows:
  - a route across the swamp;
  - a fresh-water spring;
  - a stronghold or stockade;
  - cornfields that were found but not fully investigated;
  - a beach with coconuts suitable for rendezvous and food gathering.
- The campaign reconstruction includes coconut palms, giant crab hazards, the overgrown fields, Arron Ivy's stockade, the spring, and a fixed spyglass aimed toward Riptide Cove.

**Coconut Beach and route changes**

- A beach with coconuts is discovered during the earlier island period.
- The later Arodus 5 route uses this prior knowledge and treats Coconut Beach as a ship rendezvous point.
- It is not yet secure whether Harvus' recorded beach is Coconut Beach, Crab Palm Beach, or another shoreline.
- Do not merge the later Syl-led route into the Arodus 1-4 chronology except as evidence that Rak had already learned these locations.

**Unresolved**

- Identity and fate of the lone humanoid.
- Exact order of the beach, fields, stockade, spring, and spyglass.
- Whether the giant crab encounter occurred and where it fits.
- What food and water the party actually collects before the first Riptide attempt.
- Where Rak obtains the spyglass and written island notes later associated with his inventory.

## Confirmed Sequence

1. The Erastus 31 storm carries the Man's Promise toward Bonewrack; Sandara and Tilly are taken, and grindylows fight the crew openly.
2. On Arodus 1, the ship grounds on the reef. Fresh water is lost, repairs will take roughly two days, and a four-PC supply party is sent ashore.
3. Rak, Sho, Mirei, and Harvus land, follow tracks inland, cross dangerous marsh terrain, and encounter frog threats.
4. The party rests. Mirei secretly places an alchemist's fire in a tree.
5. Ghouls attack the first island camp at night.
6. The group continues through swamp and insect hazards; Sho and Harvus are badly hurt and Harvus nearly dies.
7. The foul tree or rotting camp is found and burned.
8. The party discovers enough of the beach, field, stockade, spring, and Riptide route for Rak to guide later movement and report supply possibilities.
9. Sandara's tricorne confirms the captives' connection to Riptide Cove.
10. The first Riptide Cove assault fails. Sho goes down, Harvus is badly wounded, and the party retreats.
11. After regrouping, Rak and Sho return toward the Man's Promise. Harvus and Mirei prepare to return to Riptide Cove.
12. During the island absence, Goyle is captured in a later grindylow attack after Plugg knocks him overboard with the Tidal Cutlass.
13. Harvus and Mirei re-enter Riptide Cove through the sea entrance.
14. Harvus kills the Whale with Rak's spear; the Brinebrood Queen flees.
15. Harvus and Mirei rescue Sandara, Tilly, and Goyle.
16. The rescue is complete immediately before the preserved Arodus 5 rescue-side scene begins.
