## Question

The final session repeatedly labels its active branches `Aeodus 5th`, but the reconstructed island sequence appears to reach the same branches on Arodus 4 without an additional recorded day.

This audit separates:

- the historical session label;
- the within-day branch times;
- the independently preserved event sequence;
- and current GM correction about historical date-entry reliability.

## Pathfinder Time Basis

Pathfinder First Edition uses ordinary seconds, minutes, hours, and days.

Relevant rules structure:

- one combat round equals 6 seconds;
- ten rounds equal one minute;
- local travel is measured per minute;
- overland travel is measured per hour or per day;
- a normal overland travel day represents 8 hours of actual walking;
- a sailing ship's travel day can represent 24 hours of movement;
- natural healing normally follows 8 hours of sleep or rest;
- bards regain daily spell allotments after 8 hours of rest and 15 minutes of concentration.

PF1 does not use the later D&D 5e term `long rest` as its universal recovery mechanic, but the campaign's use of an approximately eight-hour recovery block is compatible with the underlying rules.

A rest need not begin at 10 PM or end at 6 AM. Exact clock time should follow table events, injury, interruption, daylight, and travel rather than a fixed modern sleep schedule.

## Locked Rest and Encounter Sequence

Current GM clarification and surviving evidence support three recovery periods:

1. **Initial island camp**
   - follows the first landing and early frog / mire encounter;
   - includes Mirei's hidden alchemist's-fire placement;
   - is interrupted by the third-watch ghoul attack.

2. **Coconut Beach recovery**
   - follows the swamp, swarm, and rotting-camp sequence;
   - occurs because of accumulated damage, not because conventional nightfall forced a stop;
   - is a full recovery period at Coconut Beach.

3. **Post-Riptide recovery**
   - follows the failed first Riptide Cove assault;
   - Harvus falls unconscious, later wakes near the cliff, and the party sleeps as darkness comes on;
   - the party splits after this rest.

There is no separate stockade rest.

The stockade / spring / spyglass sequence and first Riptide Cove assault occur during the same active period.

## Evidence Register

### A. Arodus 1 grounding and first camp

**Strength:** High.

- GM short-form chronology places grounding and first island departure on Arodus 1.
- Raw chat establishes landing, early encounter, camp, hidden flask, later combat, and subsequent swarm march.
- The later combat is best identified as the GM-locked ghoul attack.

### B. Harvus' independent relative day sequence

**Strength:** High for relative sequence; medium-high for date mapping.

Harvus' preserved note sequence records:

- landing, frogs, camp, and ghouls;
- `Day 29`: swamp, mosquitoes, swarm, rotting camp, beach, and camp;
- `Day 30`: first Riptide Cove assault, failure, retreat, unconsciousness, and camp;
- `Day 31`: return with Mirei and the Cauldron rescue.

If the landing sequence is Arodus 1, the most direct mapping is:

- Harvus Day 28 / landing sequence = Arodus 1;
- Day 29 = Arodus 2;
- Day 30 = Arodus 3;
- Day 31 = Arodus 4.

This is the strongest independent reason to suspect the later `Aeodus 5th` label is one day high.

### C. Current GM memory of the island days

**Strength:** Highest under the archive evidence hierarchy, but still expressed as a reconstruction rather than a final retcon.

Current GM clarification establishes:

- Coconut Beach as the post-swarm rest site;
- stockade and failed assault in one active day;
- a night rest after the failed assault;
- historical date labels were often entered one day early or late;
- the final-session day label is more suspect than the within-day minute tracking.

### D. Raw final-session label

**Strength:** Direct evidence of the historical table label; medium confidence as objective calendar truth.

The final log repeatedly says `Aeodus 5th` across the Rak and Sho branches.

This proves the session was labeled as the fifth during play. It does not prove the label was calendar-correct, because the GM has identified a recurring plus-or-minus-one-day dating problem in historical logs.

### E. Final-session branch times

**Strength:** Medium-high within branches.

The GM intentionally moved among branches out of chronological reveal order for dramatic purposes.

Therefore:

- the out-of-order pasted transcript does not invalidate the minute stamps;
- the branch times can be retained if the date changes from Arodus 5 to Arodus 4;
- isolated AM/PM mistakes remain transcription errors.

### F. Goyle's capture estimate

**Strength:** Character report.

Goyle says he was knocked from the ship about a day after the PCs set out.

This places his capture during the island expedition but does not distinguish Arodus 4 from Arodus 5 for the final rescue.

## Arodus 4 Reconstruction

The following model requires no unrecorded extra day or fourth rest:

### Arodus 1

- Man's Promise grounds.
- Supply team departs late.
- Early mire / frog encounter.
- First camp.
- Mirei hides the flask.
- Third-watch ghoul attack.
- The party completes enough interrupted rest to continue.

### Arodus 2

- Swamp and swarm travel.
- Rotting camp found and burned.
- Party reaches Coconut Beach.
- Accumulated damage forces an eight-hour recovery period.

The rest can begin whenever the party reaches the beach. It need not be a conventional overnight block.

### Arodus 3

- Party leaves Coconut Beach after recovery and usable-light delay as needed.
- Stockade, spring, and spyglass sequence.
- First Riptide Cove assault.
- Failed retreat.
- Harvus loses consciousness.
- Party camps and sleeps as darkness comes on.

### Arodus 4

- Party splits after the post-assault rest.
- Rak and Sho return toward the Man's Promise.
- Harvus and Mirei return to Riptide Cove.
- Successful Cauldron rescue occurs.
- Rak is questioned by Scourge and sent back out with Syl's supply party.
- Sho's confinement branch proceeds using the recorded late-morning and midday times.
- Rak's route proceeds through the recorded afternoon times.

This model matches the three-rest sequence and Harvus' Day 28-31 relative chronology.

## What Arodus 5 Would Require

Retaining Arodus 5 as objective calendar truth requires at least one of the following:

- an unrecorded additional full day between the Coconut Beach rest and stockade departure;
- an unrecorded additional full day after the failed assault;
- a landing or first camp date later than Arodus 1;
- Harvus' Day 29-31 labels not representing consecutive active days;
- or another substantial delay not currently preserved.

None of these is impossible, but none is presently supported strongly enough to be preferred over the Arodus 4 mapping.
