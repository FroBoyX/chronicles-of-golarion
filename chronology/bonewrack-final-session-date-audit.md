## Audit Question

The live final-session log repeatedly labels its active branches `Aeodus 5th`, while the independently preserved event sequence reaches those branches on the fourth island day after the Arodus 1 landing.

The audit separated:

- historical source labels;
- within-day branch times;
- independently preserved relative-day sequences;
- rest structure;
- and current GM correction about historical date-entry reliability.

## Pathfinder Time Basis

Pathfinder First Edition uses ordinary seconds, minutes, hours, and days.

Relevant structure:

- one combat round equals 6 seconds;
- ten rounds equal one minute;
- local travel is measured per minute;
- overland travel is measured per hour or day;
- a normal travel day represents roughly 8 hours of walking;
- natural healing and many daily spell-recovery procedures use approximately 8 hours of sleep or rest.

PF1 does not require every recovery period to run from a conventional modern bedtime to morning. The table's injury-driven recovery blocks can begin when the party becomes unable to continue.

## Locked Rest Structure

The expedition contains three recovery periods:

1. **First island camp** after landing, interrupted by the undead attack.
2. **Coconut Beach-area recovery** after the swamp and swarm damage.
3. **Burned-tree camp** after the failed Riptide Cove assault.

There is no fourth rest and no separate stockade rest.

The stockade, spring, spyglass, and first Riptide assault occur during the same active Arodus 3 period.

## Evidence Register

### Arodus 1 anchor

- The Man's Promise grounds and the island party departs on Arodus 1.
- Direct chat and player narratives place the frog encounter, first camp, hidden flask, and undead attack in that first island period.

### Harvus relative-day sequence

Harvus preserves:

- Day 28: landing, frogs, first camp, undead;
- Day 29: swamp, swarms, burning site, beach, recovery;
- Day 30: first Riptide assault, retreat, burned-tree camp;
- Day 31: successful return, rescue, aftermath, and movement toward the fork.

His later heading `Arodus 5th, 4701 (Day 31 pt. 2)` contains a demonstrably incorrect campaign year and directly continues Day 31. Its metadata is unreliable even though its event sequence is valuable.

### Sho relative-day sequence

Sho independently preserves:

- Day 28;
- Day 29;
- a missing-heading Day 30 cave fragment;
- Day 31 morning split and village return.

### Rak relative-day sequence

Rak's deliberately edited report to Scourge divides the expedition into:

- first day;
- second day;
- third day;
- `today`, when Rak and Sho return to the village.

The report's factual claims are self-serving and filtered, but the four-day count independently matches Harvus and Sho.

### Final-session timestamps

The GM deliberately moved among parallel branches out of chronological reveal order.

Therefore:

- timestamps remain useful within each branch;
- pasted transcript order is not a synchronized master clock;
- isolated AM/PM errors remain transcription noise;
- changing the date from Arodus 5 to Arodus 4 does not invalidate the within-day branch times.

### Village return and escort evidence

Rak and Sho reach the original village on the morning after the burned-tree camp. Their travel from the village to the Man's Promise is uneventful.

Rak is later questioned and sent back across Bonewrack with Syl, Jape, Shivikah, and Fipps. No missing travel encounter, rest, or hidden day exists between the village and ship.

### Goyle's report

Goyle says he was knocked from the ship about a day after the PCs set out. This supports a later separate capture but does not create an additional island day.

## Final Ruling

The final Bonewrack split-session branches occurred on **Arodus 4, 4712 AR**.

Preserve `Aeodus 5th`, `Arodus 5`, and `Arodus 5th, 4701` only as historical source labels or quoted errors.

Do not reopen the calendar unless new primary evidence introduces a genuine additional day boundary. Routine map, item, and initiative questions do not affect the date.
