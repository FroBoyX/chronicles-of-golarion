- `Day 28 (I think)` for landing, destroyed bridge, frog encounter, first camp, and undead attack;
- `Day 29 (Im fairly certain)` for jungle, swamp, swarms, fire, Coconut Beach, coconuts, and a beach-area camp;
- an unlabeled cave fragment between Day 29 and Day 31, best identified as Day 30, for the first Riptide assault, retreat, and burned-tree camp;
- `Day 31 (No one's correcting me so its gotta be right)` for the morning split and village return.

This exactly matches the relative four-day structure independently preserved by:

- Harvus' Day 28-31 notes;
- Rak's `First day / Second day / Third day / Today` report structure.

With Arodus 1 locked as the grounding and first landing day, all three character-side structures map to:

- **Arodus 1:** landing, bridge / frogs, first camp, ghouls;
- **Arodus 2:** swamp, swarms, Coconut Beach, recovery;
- **Arodus 3:** first Riptide assault, retreat, burned-tree camp;
- **Arodus 4:** morning split, village return, rescue-side return, Sho punishment, and Rak reassignment.

Sho's own uncertainty language should be preserved as player voice, but the convergence of three independent structures makes the repeated final-session `Aeodus 5th` label substantially weaker as objective calendar truth.
