## Rescue Return Branch — After Riptide Cove

### Harvus Group

Harvus, Mirei, Sandara, Tilly, and Goyle emerge from the Riptide Cove rescue sequence.

- Sandara coughs water and remains unconscious or mostly unresponsive.
- Tilly says Sandara took the worst of the grindylow abuse.
- Tilly collapses from her own wounds after surfacing.
- Goyle surfaces alive while removing a tentacle fragment from his arm.
- Harvus helps Goyle ashore.
- Goyle warns the group not to expect a warm welcome from Plugg and Scourge.
- Goyle says Plugg blasted him and a grindylow off the ship with the Tidal Cutlass.
- Goyle says Plugg is no pirate captain and warns that Plugg and Scourge meant to eliminate the PCs if they returned.
- Mirei confesses that Plugg tasked her with killing Rak, Sho, and Harvus to prove loyalty, but she did not act on it.
- Harvus carries Sandara; Goyle supports Tilly.
- The group skirts the cornfield and moves toward the fork.

This branch knows Goyle's accusation and Mirei's confession. Rak and Sho do not know either before communication occurs.

## 11:45 AM — Scourge Questions Rak

Master Scourge questions Rak about food, water, and the island route.

Rak explains the broken-bridge route and the available food and water discoveries.

Scourge revises the plan:

- Slippery Syl leads the supply team.
- Jape, Shivikah, Fipps, and Rak accompany her.
- They are not to stop at the caverns.
- The ship will sail and meet them at the south / coconut beach.
- They must bring spring water down to the beach.
- They must recover as much food as possible.

## Late Morning / Midday — Broken Bridge and Frogs

Rak leads the group toward the broken bridge.

- Syl orders Rak to cross first because he has crossed before.
- Rak falls in during the crossing.
- Frogs attack Rak and Fipps.
- Shivikah dives in to help Rak and cuts or stabs the frog holding him.
- Fipps panics and flails in the water.
- Syl uses axe, cutlass, and command pressure to keep the party moving.
- Jape lands the final hit before becoming stuck or beginning to sink.
- The group survives but is injured and more tense.

Shivikah's aid is visible to Rak but does not automatically resolve her loyalty.

## Midday — Botflies and Formation

The party encounters signs of the insect swarms Rak warned about.

Rak wants to move quickly. Syl stops him and says they move as a group because she needs him alive.

She distributes flasks and establishes formation:

- Jape in the lead;
- Fipps in the middle;
- Shivikah guarding Fipps;
- Rak at the rear.

This establishes Syl as tactically capable as well as violent.

## 12:21–12:47 PM — Sho and Kahina

Sho calls out from captivity aboard the Man's Promise. Kahina Bukekayo answers.

- Kahina identifies herself as Sho's guard in the scene.
- Kahina says the ship is setting sail for the far shore to take on supplies and then will leave for an unknown destination.
- Kahina confronts Sho over the Man's Promise dead.
- Sho expresses remorse and says she wants to live long enough to kill the people in command.
- Kahina says words mean nothing and demands a life-for-life test.
- Kahina asks whether Sho will kill those who torment them and risk her own life to end theirs.
- Kahina warns that if Sho hesitates, Kahina will ensure Sho dies for her sins.
- Sho agrees.
- Kahina leaves.

The exact guard state after Kahina leaves remains unresolved.

## 12:28 PM — Burned Tree

Rak's group reaches the burned-tree landmark.

- Syl asks what happened there.
- Rak answers with alchemy-themed humor.
- Syl refuses to stop and keeps the group moving.

## 1:30 PM — Cornfield Caution

Rak recommends caution before the unexplored cornfields.

- Syl refuses to rest before the stronghold.
- Fipps is visibly exhausted and sits during the pause.
- Syl threatens Rak over insubordination and reminds him she needs him alive, not his opinions.
- Rak argues that being needed alive justifies moving cautiously.
- Shivikah appears nervous.
- Jape appears disinterested.
- The group continues.

## 2:15–2:25 PM — Coconut Beach

Rak's group reaches and passes Coconut Beach.

- Rak points out the coconut palms.
- Syl says they are going to the stronghold and that the ship should meet them at the beach.
- Syl orders Fipps to remain at Coconut Beach.
- Rak, Syl, Shivikah, and Jape continue inland.
- Fipps is no longer part of the moving fork group.

## 2:27 PM — Last Checkpoint

The source uses the label `survivors` for a group pausing at its last checkpoint while watching approaching botflies.

The label is not securely assigned to one branch. Do not use it to merge the rescue group and Rak group before the fork collision.

Rak hears something ahead on the path.

The rescue group is approaching the same fork from its own route.

This is the immediate collision point before the runnable fork encounter. Stop here; do not predetermine recognition, initiative, surrender, or casualties in this chronology.
