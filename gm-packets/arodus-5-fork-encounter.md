## Call-Back Summary

This is not a mutiny negotiation.

This is the moment Plugg and Scourge's execution plan becomes visible and collapses into open violence.

Visible recognition establishes only what the two groups can see:

- Harvus survived.
- Mirei returned with him instead of completing her assigned betrayal.
- Sandara and Tilly survived the grindylows.
- Goyle survived and is with the rescue group.
- Sandara is unconscious and cannot mediate.

Syl does not begin with the rescue group's private knowledge. She does not automatically know Goyle's accusation, the details of Mirei's confession, the Whale's death, the Queen's escape, or the lost item state. She can infer that the returning group is dangerous to Plugg's position, but specific facts must be revealed through recognition, dialogue, or combat.

Syl and Jape fight until physically incapable or dead. Shivikah begins on their side but may surrender when defeat becomes undeniable. Syl assumes Rak remains aligned with her group until he attacks Syl, Jape, or Shivikah, or openly moves to defend the returning group.

Expected duration: two to four rounds. A faster resolution is acceptable.

## Encounter Purpose

The encounter should accomplish five things quickly:

1. Expose that Harvus was supposed to die.
2. Expose Mirei's failure to complete her assigned betrayal.
3. Force Rak to choose a side in public.
4. Remove, kill, capture, or break the inland Plugg-loyalist group.
5. Create the casualties and distrust that make later shipboard survival politics urgent.

Do not slow the scene down to preserve future crew. The later pragmatism exists because this fight is lethal.

Initiative begins when any of the following occurs:

- Syl draws or signals Jape to attack Harvus.
- Jape advances with lethal intent.
- A PC attacks, draws, casts, moves to intercept, or attempts to withdraw tactically.
- Goyle releases or shifts Tilly and interposes with a weapon.
- Rak openly declares against Syl's group.

There is no stable extended negotiation. Syl may answer one sentence, but visual proof that Harvus and Mirei returned has already collapsed the covert plan.

## NPC Priorities

### Slippery Syl Lonegan

#### Objective Order

1. Kill Harvus.
2. Stop Mirei from returning to the ship.
3. Kill or disable immediate threats protecting Harvus.
4. Keep Rak neutral unless he turns against her.
5. Prevent Goyle from escaping if he openly reveals Plugg's betrayal.

#### Behavior

- Syl directly confronts Mirei over Harvus being alive.
- She treats Rak as part of her side until he proves otherwise.
- She focuses lethal force rather than spreading damage.
- She attempts to finish Harvus if he falls and no immediate threat prevents it.
- She fights until incapable or dead.
- She does not voluntarily surrender.
- She does not retreat merely to preserve future crew viability.

#### Target Changes

- If Rak attacks her group, Rak becomes an immediate enemy.
- If Goyle blocks access to Harvus, Syl may attack Goyle to clear the path.
- If Mirei becomes the greatest immediate threat, Syl turns on Mirei without abandoning the execution objective.
- Syl does not begin by attacking unconscious Sandara or weakened Tilly unless they directly interfere or become tactically necessary targets.

### Jaundiced Jape

#### Objective Order

1. Obey Syl's direction and attack Harvus.
2. Commit to whichever PC or defender prevents him from reaching that target.
3. Continue fighting until physically incapable or dead.

#### Behavior

- Jape does not negotiate.
- Jape does not surrender.
- Jape does not need dialogue.
- Once engaged, he commits fully to his opponent and tries to take that opponent down with him.
- If Harvus falls, Jape attacks the nearest PC threat rather than seeking out unconscious Sandara or weakened Tilly.
- If Syl falls, Jape continues fighting.

### Shivikah

#### Initial Position

- Shivikah begins on Syl's side.
- She follows the order to keep the rescue group from interfering with Syl and Jape's targets.
- She does not begin with rescue-side knowledge.
- She is not eager to die for Plugg.
- She does not begin by targeting unconscious Sandara or injured Tilly unless Tilly attacks or directly blocks her.

#### Preferred Behavior

- Block Goyle, Mirei, or another defender.
- Defend herself and the current side while it appears viable.
- Avoid suicidal pursuit.
- Do not finish downed enemies unless directly ordered and still convinced Syl controls the field.

#### Surrender and Morale Conditions

Shivikah may surrender when one of these becomes true:

- Syl and Jape are both incapable.
- Syl is dead or incapable, Jape is clearly losing, and Shivikah is isolated.
- Shivikah is badly wounded and offered a credible surrender.
- Rak has openly turned, the rescue group controls the field, and continued resistance is plainly suicidal.

Her surrender is survival, not conversion. She drops or throws aside her weapon, backs away, and says something direct:

> "I'm done."

or:

> "Not dying for them."

She does not apologize, immediately become friendly, or volunteer hidden information she does not possess.

### Goyle Onorach

#### Objective Order

1. Keep Tilly on her feet or get her safely down.
2. Protect Sandara once Harvus must fight or put her down.
3. Stop Syl or Jape from finishing Harvus or Mirei.
4. Tell Rak the truth if Rak appears uncertain.

#### Behavior

- Goyle takes the conscious protective role Sandara cannot fill.
- He begins physically supporting Tilly, not already standing in a perfect defensive formation.
- He does not attempt a long peace speech.
- He has cast his lot with Harvus and Mirei after the rescue.
- He fights defensively around Tilly and Sandara unless an opening or finishing threat demands action.
- If Harvus falls, Goyle prioritizes preventing a killing blow.

### Tilly Brackett

#### Objective Order

1. Remain standing or get Sandara out of immediate danger.
2. Use Harvus's flail to defend herself, Sandara, or Goyle if directly threatened.
3. Help stabilize, drag, or shield a fallen ally when possible.

#### Behavior

- Tilly is injured and supported by Goyle at the opening.
- She is armed, but she should not be treated as a fresh offensive combatant.
- Whether she can fight effectively, only defend, or must be placed safely is a GM staging decision based on her exact condition.

### Sandara Quinn

- Sandara remains unconscious or barely conscious.
- She cannot stop or mediate the fight.
- She does not have her tricorne.
- Do not display or use her holy symbol until its holder is resolved.
- Her vulnerability drives Harvus, Goyle, and Tilly's immediate choices.

## Rak's Decision Point

Syl assumes Rak remains on her side.

Rak becomes an enemy only when he:

- attacks Syl, Jape, or Shivikah;
- openly declares for Harvus and Mirei;
- moves to block Syl or Jape;
- attempts to disarm or restrain one of them;
- warns the rescue group and acts on that warning;
- or otherwise directly interferes with the execution.

Merely hesitating does not immediately make him a target. Syl would rather keep him out of the fight and return him to the ship while he remains useful.

Do not tell Rak that his spear is lost as GM narration. He can learn it from Harvus or Mirei after communication, notice that Harvus no longer has it, or ask directly.

## Expected Fight Shape

Expected length: two to four rounds.

Expected pattern:

1. Visual recognition exposes Harvus and Mirei's return.
2. Syl and Jape focus Harvus or whoever directly protects him.
3. Harvus must decide how and where to put down or protect Sandara.
4. Goyle must decide whether to keep supporting Tilly or release her to defend the group.
5. Rak declares himself through action.
6. Shivikah fights while Syl's side appears viable.
7. Syl and Jape continue until incapable or dead.
8. Shivikah surrenders if the field turns decisively against her.

The fight may end extremely quickly. Do not inflate enemy durability or delay surrender solely to create a longer set piece.

## Likely Outcomes

### Most Likely

- Syl dies or is rendered incapable.
- Jape dies or is rendered incapable.
- Shivikah surrenders.
- One or more PCs or allied NPCs are badly injured.
- A PC or allied NPC may die if Syl and Jape successfully focus a target.

### If Shivikah Also Dies

Treat it as the consequence of combat momentum, a failed surrender opportunity, or player choice. Do not retroactively make her a committed Plugg loyalist merely because she died beside Syl and Jape.

### If Syl or Jape Is Captured Alive

They remain committed enemies. Restraint, guarding, transport, and eventual judgment become immediate problems. Neither becomes cooperative merely because they lost.

### If Rak Supports Syl

The encounter becomes a genuine PC-versus-PC lethal crisis. Do not protect the assumed future campaign structure from that decision. Goyle, Tilly, and the rescue group understand Rak as participating in the execution attempt.

### If Rak Remains Neutral

Syl accepts his noninterference during the fight. The rescue group may not. His refusal to act becomes a later political fact even if he never attacks them.
