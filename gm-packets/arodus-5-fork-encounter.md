## Call-Back Summary

This is not a mutiny negotiation.

This is the moment Plugg and Scourge's execution plan becomes visible and collapses into open violence.

Syl recognizes immediately that:

- Harvus survived.
- Mirei failed to kill the other PCs.
- Goyle survived and can testify against Plugg.
- Sandara and Tilly survived the grindylows.
- The returning group cannot be allowed to reach the ship without destroying Plugg's cover.

Syl and Jape fight until incapable or dead. Shivikah begins on their side but surrenders if defeat becomes undeniable. Syl assumes Rak remains compliant until he directly attacks Syl, Jape, or Shivikah, or openly moves to defend the returning group.

Sandara is unconscious and cannot serve as the voice of reason. Goyle becomes the conscious protector of Sandara and Tilly.

Expected duration: two to four rounds.

## Encounter Purpose

The encounter should accomplish five things quickly:

1. Expose that Harvus was supposed to die.
2. Expose Mirei's failure to complete her assigned murder.
3. Force Rak to choose a side in public.
4. Remove or break the inland Plugg-loyalist group.
5. Create casualties that make later shipboard survival and crew-count politics urgent.

Do not slow the scene down to preserve future crew. The later pragmatism exists because this fight is lethal.

## Default Staging

Use the current map if one exists.

If no tactical map fixes the positions:

- Rak's party is moving in a loose trail formation.
- Rak is forward enough to hear the returning group first.
- Syl is close behind Rak.
- Jape and Shivikah are within one move of Rak and Syl.
- Harvus appears carrying or supporting Sandara.
- Mirei and Goyle are able to move between the hostile party and Tilly/Sandara.
- Tilly remains close to Sandara.

Do not grant a surprise round by default. Rak hears movement, and the groups recognize each other when they come into view. If a player interrupts the opening by attacking, drawing, hiding, or moving, call initiative immediately and resolve that declared action under the normal initiative rules.

## Initiative Trigger

Initiative begins when any of the following occurs:

- Syl draws and orders Jape onto Harvus.
- A PC attacks or moves to attack.
- Goyle draws or interposes with clear hostile intent.
- Rak openly declares against Syl.

There is no meaningful extended negotiation. Syl may answer one sentence, but she is already committed to lethal force.

## NPC Combat Directives

## Slippery Syl Lonegan

### Objective Order

1. Kill Harvus.
2. Stop Mirei from escaping or reaching the ship.
3. Kill or disable any immediate threat protecting Harvus.
4. Keep Rak neutral unless he turns against her.
5. Prevent Goyle from escaping with testimony if the opportunity presents itself.

### Behavior

- Syl has no pretense left once she sees Harvus alive.
- She calls Mirei out directly for failing her assignment.
- She treats Rak as part of her side until he proves otherwise.
- She uses focused, lethal tactics rather than spreading damage.
- She attempts to finish Harvus if he falls and no more immediate threat prevents it.
- She fights until incapable or dead.
- She does not surrender.
- She does not retreat toward the ship if doing so would leave Harvus, Mirei, or Goyle alive to expose the plan.

### Target Changes

- If Rak attacks her, Rak becomes an immediate target.
- If Goyle blocks access to Harvus, Syl may attack Goyle to clear the path.
- If Mirei becomes the most immediate threat, Syl turns on Mirei without abandoning the execution objective.

## Jaundiced Jape

### Objective Order

1. Obey Syl's order to attack Harvus.
2. Attack the nearest PC preventing that objective.
3. Continue fighting until incapable or dead.

### Behavior

- Jape does not negotiate.
- Jape does not surrender.
- Jape does not need dialogue.
- Jape commits fully once Syl gives the order.
- If Harvus falls, Jape attacks the next PC threat rather than shifting toward Tilly or unconscious Sandara unless they directly interfere.
- If Syl falls, Jape continues fighting.

## Shivikah

### Initial Position

- Shivikah begins on Syl's side.
- She obeys the order to keep the others away from Syl and Jape's targets.
- She is not eager to die for Plugg.
- She does not begin by targeting unconscious Sandara or weakened Tilly unless one becomes an active combatant.

### Preferred Behavior

- Block Goyle, Mirei, or another defender from interfering.
- Defend herself and her current side.
- Avoid suicidal pursuit.
- Do not finish downed enemies unless directly ordered and still convinced Syl controls the field.

### Surrender Triggers

Shivikah may surrender when one of these becomes true:

- Syl and Jape are both incapable.
- Syl is dead or incapable, Jape is clearly losing, and Shivikah is isolated.
- Shivikah is badly wounded and offered a credible surrender.
- Rak has openly turned, the returning group controls the field, and continued resistance is plainly suicidal.

### Surrender Action

Shivikah drops or throws aside her weapon, backs away, and says something direct such as:

> "I'm done."

or:

> "Not dying for them."

She does not need to apologize or become friendly. Her surrender is survival, not conversion.

## Goyle Onórach

### Objective Order

1. Protect unconscious Sandara.
2. Protect Tilly.
3. Stop Syl or Jape from finishing Harvus or Mirei.
4. Tell Rak the truth if Rak appears uncertain.

### Behavior

- Goyle takes the conscious protector role Sandara cannot fill.
- He does not attempt a long peace speech.
- He already believes Plugg's faction intended an ambush and has cast his lot with Harvus' group.
- He fights defensively around Sandara and Tilly unless an opening demands action.
- If Harvus falls, Goyle prioritizes preventing a killing blow.

## Tilly Brackett

### Objective Order

1. Keep Sandara out of immediate danger.
2. Move Sandara if the battlefield shifts onto them.
3. Defend herself if cornered.

### Behavior

- Tilly is weak and exhausted.
- She should not become a primary offensive combatant unless forced.
- She may assist, drag, stabilize, or shield rather than attack.

## Sandara Quinn

- Sandara remains unconscious or nearly unresponsive.
- She cannot stop the fight.
- Do not use her as the voice of reason during this encounter.
- Her vulnerability increases Goyle and Tilly's urgency.

## Rak's Decision Point

Syl assumes Rak is still on her side.

Rak becomes an enemy only when he:

- attacks Syl, Jape, or Shivikah;
- openly declares for Harvus and Mirei;
- moves to block Syl or Jape;
- attempts to disarm or restrain one of them;
- or otherwise directly interferes with the execution.

Merely hesitating does not immediately make him a target. Syl would rather keep him out of the fight and return him to the ship while he remains useful.

This should create a visible player decision rather than forcing Rak into either faction before he acts.

## Expected Fight Shape

Expected length: two to four rounds.

Expected pattern:

1. Syl and Jape focus Harvus or whoever directly protects him.
2. Goyle moves to defend Sandara, Tilly, and any fallen member of the returning group.
3. Rak declares himself through action.
4. Shivikah fights while Syl's side appears viable.
5. Syl and Jape continue until incapable or dead.
6. Shivikah surrenders if the field turns decisively against her.

The fight may end very quickly. That is acceptable. Do not inflate enemy durability solely to prolong it.

## Likely Outcomes

### Most Likely

- Syl dies or is rendered incapable.
- Jape dies or is rendered incapable.
- Shivikah surrenders.
- One or more PCs or allied NPCs are badly injured.
- A PC or allied NPC may die if Syl and Jape successfully focus their target.

### If Shivikah Also Dies

Treat it as the consequence of momentum, failed surrender opportunity, or the PCs' choices. Do not retroactively make her a committed Plugg loyalist merely because she died with them.

### If Syl or Jape Is Captured Alive

They remain committed enemies. Restraint, guarding, transport, and eventual judgment become immediate problems. Neither should become cooperative because they lost.

## Casualty Accounting

The Man's Promise storm-onset complement is 27.

| Fork result | Crew remaining before later Plugg / Scourge conflict |
|---|---:|
| Syl and Jape die; Shivikah survives | 25 |
| Syl, Jape, and Shivikah die | 24 |
| Two loyalists and one allied character die | 24 |
| Three loyalists and one allied character die | 23 |
| Three loyalists and two allied characters die | 22 |

Later removal of Plugg and Scourge reduces those totals by another two.

This means:

- If only Syl and Jape die, the ship has some later casualty margin.
- If Shivikah and allied characters also die, the final conflict can push the survivors to or below the effective Crew 20 threshold.
- The later survivor-bloc pragmatism should emerge after this fight, not constrain it beforehand.

## Immediate Aftermath

Do not jump directly to the beach or shipboard mutiny.

Resolve these beats first:

1. Stabilize the dying.
2. Confirm who is dead, unconscious, or capable of walking.
3. Resolve Shivikah's surrender, if applicable.
4. Allow Goyle to state the ambush truth plainly.
5. Allow Mirei's failed murder assignment to become public if it was not already exposed.
6. Decide whether Sandara wakes or remains unconscious.
7. Decide who carries the wounded.
8. Determine whether the group continues toward Coconut Beach together.
9. Update the viable Man's Promise crew count.

## Information Revealed by the Encounter

After the fight, the combined group can reasonably know:

- Plugg and Scourge expected the island to kill the PCs.
- They planned to kill returning PCs if the island failed.
- Mirei was tasked with killing the others and refused.
- Goyle accuses Plugg of deliberately knocking him overboard with the Tidal Cutlass.
- Sho may already be in danger aboard the ship, though the group does not yet know the Kahina bargain.

Do not reveal Sho and Kahina's private exchange through this scene.

## Do Not Do

- Do not use the line "No witnesses."
- Do not imply Syl intends to kill Rak before he turns against her.
- Do not force a long villain speech before players can react.
- Do not let Sandara mediate while unconscious.
- Do not make Shivikah surrender before the tide clearly turns.
- Do not make Syl or Jape surrender for later crew viability.
- Do not add the Brinebrood Queen to this fight.
- Do not resolve permanent ownership or captaincy of the Man's Promise here.
