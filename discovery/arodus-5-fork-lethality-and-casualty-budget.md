# Arodus 5 Fork Lethality and Casualty Budget

## Document Status

- Type: GM discovery / encounter-design note
- Maturity: Working discovery
- Scope: Immediate Arodus 5 fork collision, Plugg / Scourge kill plan, likely casualties, and downstream sailing viability
- Canon status: The kill plan and branch evidence are established; exact combat sequence and casualties are not canon until played

## Core Correction

The Arodus 5 fork should not be designed as an early version of the later shipboard political negotiation.

It is the collision point of an attempted execution plan.

Plugg and Scourge intended the three other PCs to die:

- Sho has already been beaten unconscious and confined aboard the Man's Promise.
- Rak is being kept alive because Syl needs him to guide the supply party through the island; Syl explicitly says he would already be dead if she did not need him.
- Harvus was expected to die on the island or be killed if he returned.
- Mirei was tasked with killing the other PCs to prove loyalty and refused.

The rescue-side group knows enough to expect an ambush. Goyle overheard Plugg's faction discussing killing the PCs if the island did not kill them first, and Mirei has confessed her assigned murder task.

When the branches meet, the situation is therefore not:

> Which faction should everyone support?

It is:

> The designated victims have returned alive, together, carrying witnesses, and the execution team cannot allow them to reach the ship.

## The Three Likely Plugg-Side Deaths

The likely three hostile deaths at the fork are:

- Slippery Syl Lonegan
- Jaundiced Jape
- Shivikah

This is a combat-side classification, not necessarily a statement that all three are equally ideologically loyal to Plugg.

### Slippery Syl

Syl is the clear execution-side commander.

- She holds delegated authority from Plugg / Scourge.
- She repeatedly threatens Rak.
- She states that Rak would already be dead if she did not need him alive.
- The return of Harvus, Mirei, Sandara, Tilly, and Goyle destroys the possibility that the island quietly solved Plugg's problem.
- Goyle is a direct witness against Plugg.
- Mirei's presence proves she rejected the murder order.

Syl has strong reasons to initiate lethal force and little reason to allow the group to reach Coconut Beach or the ship.

### Jaundiced Jape

Jape is the most straightforward loyalist combat asset.

- He operates within Syl's command structure.
- He has functioned as silent hostile pressure.
- He is unlikely to lead negotiation or independently re-evaluate the wider politics during a sudden collision.

Once Syl attacks, Jape should be expected to fight lethally unless incapacitated or clearly defeated.

### Shivikah

Shivikah is more complicated.

- She helped Rak during the frog fight.
- She looked nervous during Syl's confrontation with Rak.
- Her ideological loyalty to Plugg remains less certain than Syl's or Jape's.

However, she is still positioned inside the execution-side party when the branches collide. A lethal fight can make distinction irrelevant before she has time or courage to defect.

Default encounter expectation:

- She fights with Syl and Jape once violence begins.
- A player-created opening may allow hesitation, surrender, flight, or defection.
- The encounter should not depend on that opening occurring.
- If she dies with Syl and Jape, her death should remain tragic or ambiguous rather than retroactively proving deep Plugg loyalty.

## Why Negotiation Is Unlikely at the Fork

Several facts compress the decision window:

1. Goyle can expose Plugg's attempted murder and identify himself as a living witness.
2. Mirei's return exposes her refusal to carry out Plugg's order.
3. Harvus' group is carrying people Plugg's faction expected to remain dead or lost.
4. Rak is no longer uniquely necessary once the route and supplies are known or once another group can reach the beach.
5. Syl cannot safely escort this group back without exposing Plugg's plan.
6. Harvus' group already believes an ambush awaits them.

There may be a brief recognition / accusation beat, but not a stable parley.

## Recommended Encounter Shape

### Phase 1 — Recognition

Rak hears the approaching rescue group before visual contact.

The branches identify one another:

- relief and confusion from Rak / PCs,
- shock or calculation from Syl,
- alarm from Shivikah,
- hostility from Jape,
- urgency from Goyle.

### Phase 2 — Exposure

Goyle or Mirei reveals enough to make the stakes clear:

- Plugg intended the PCs to die.
- Mirei was ordered to kill the others.
- Goyle was deliberately washed overboard by Plugg.

Syl recognizes that these witnesses cannot be allowed to return.

### Phase 3 — Lethal Initiation

Syl orders or begins the attack.

This should feel like an execution team trying to complete a failed assignment, not a normal crew dispute.

Likely priorities:

- Syl targets Rak or Mirei as the exposed traitor / expendable guide.
- Jape attacks the nearest vulnerable or dangerous target.
- Shivikah follows the attack unless given a credible immediate exit.
- Harvus and Goyle understand that capture means death or return to Plugg's ambush.

### Phase 4 — Casualties and Triage

After combat, the surviving group must immediately:

- stabilize anyone who can be saved,
- determine whether Sandara and Tilly can continue,
- recover weapons and supplies,
- decide what to do with prisoners if any,
- move toward Coconut Beach before the ship arrives or before Fipps can warn it.

This is where the campaign's later manpower pragmatism begins.

## Casualty Budget

The Man's Promise has **27 associated people** at storm onset / current split accounting.

| Event | Remaining associated people |
|---|---:|
| Starting Man's Promise complement | 27 |
| Syl, Jape, and Shivikah die at fork | 24 |
| One additional PC / NPC death at fork | 23 |
| Plugg and Scourge later die | 21 |
| One further death in later conflict | 20 |
| Any additional death after that | 19 or fewer; formally under Crew 20 reference |

Alternative if no allied death occurs at the fork:

| Event | Remaining associated people |
|---|---:|
| Starting complement | 27 |
| Three Plugg-side fork deaths | 24 |
| Plugg and Scourge die later | 22 |
| One additional later death | 21 |
| Two additional later deaths | 20 |

The distinction matters:

- The fork can be lethal without the characters thinking first about future crew numbers.
- After the fork, every further death becomes visibly dangerous to escape viability.
- The survivors may only realize how narrow the margin is once they count bodies, wounded, missing people, and people unwilling to serve.

## Immediate Conflict vs Later Pragmatism

### At the fork

The operative rule is:

> Kill or be killed.

The Plugg-side party is executing a covert murder plan whose cover has collapsed. The PCs and rescue-side NPCs have direct reason to believe surrender or return under guard means death.

### At Coconut Beach / aboard the ship

The operative rule changes to:

> Stop the killing before there are too few people left to escape.

This later rule does not soften the fork retroactively. It is the consequence of surviving it.

People such as Goyle, Merchiel, Kahina, Ebony, Kroop, Sandara, Samms, Ratline, and Conchobhar can become the voices insisting that the next confrontation cannot become another uncontrolled massacre.

## Consequences for the Man's Promise Survivor Bloc

The original Man's Promise survivors should not simply celebrate the deaths of Wormwood personnel.

Likely reactions:

- **Kahina:** Syl / Jape deaths are acceptable if they were executing Plugg's plan; she still judges the PCs by whom they kill and whether they accept surrender.
- **Ebony:** counts able riggers and immediately recognizes the operational cost, especially if Shivikah dies.
- **Goyle:** accepts lethal self-defense against Plugg's execution party but pushes for discipline afterward.
- **Merchiel:** becomes more insistent that no one can afford another uncontrolled fight.
- **Dahrehn:** sees the ship's value collapsing with every dead sailor and injured specialist.
- **Nor'gal:** may consider the deaths irrelevant to his own revenge and remain dangerous to Harvus / Rak.

The survivor bloc's later demand for control or representation becomes stronger, not weaker:

> The Wormwood factions have already turned the island into another killing ground. The original crew will not allow the ship's future to be decided by whoever kills fastest.

## Consequences for the Beach Conflict

After three likely fork deaths, Plugg and Scourge can still be approached as lethal enemies, but the encounter should contain visible pressure against a general massacre.

Recommended survival outcomes for secondary hostile assets:

- Fipps may surrender, flee, or be captured rather than automatically die.
- Owlbear should remain turnable, releasable, or noncombatant if possible.
- Shipboard neutral / survivor crew should not join a mass fight unless forced.
- Plugg and Scourge may be the two people for whom the conflict remains irreducibly lethal.

This preserves the kill-or-be-killed truth of the assassination plot while preventing every later opponent from functioning identically.

## Consequences for the Brinebrood Queen

A major Queen attack that kills additional crew immediately after the fork could push the ship below viable staffing after Plugg and Scourge fall.

Therefore:

- Do not use her as another broad attrition encounter in the same sequence.
- If she appears, use a targeted revenge objective against Harvus, a boat, or a specific captive.
- Prefer injury, abduction attempt, damaged launch, or forced cooperation over several additional random deaths.
- Saving her for later remains viable if her immediate appearance would erase the human conflict or break the crew-count margin.

## GM Use Summary

- Treat the fork as the violent completion of Plugg / Scourge's murder plan.
- Default likely hostile deaths: Syl, Jape, Shivikah.
- Preserve Shivikah's ambiguity even if she dies on the hostile side.
- Do not force future-manpower pragmatism into the characters' pre-combat thinking.
- Begin the manpower crisis during post-combat triage.
- Starting at 24 after the likely three deaths, Plugg / Scourge deaths and one additional casualty leave only 21.
- Every later death should visibly narrow the crew's ability to escape Bonewrack.
