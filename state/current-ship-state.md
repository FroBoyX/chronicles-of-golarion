## Rules Reference — Man's Promise Crew Requirement

The Man's Promise is treated as a Pathfinder First Edition sailing ship for operating-crew purposes.

Working reference:

- PF1 sailing ship vehicle statistics use **Crew 20**.
- Crew 20 is the effective crew needed for proper, sustainable operation.
- Fewer than twenty people may move or reposition the ship in an emergency, but the vessel is under-crewed, unsafe, and vulnerable to failed repairs, poor watches, fatigue, bad weather, and morale collapse.

## Man's Promise

### Physical Status

The Man's Promise is damaged from the reef incident but appears to be moving or preparing to move along the Bonewrack shore by the time Sho speaks with Kahina.

Kahina tells Sho that the ship has set sail for the far shore to take on supplies and then intends to leave for an unknown destination.

Fresh water was compromised when the ship struck the reef. The ship is expected to meet the supply effort at Coconut Beach.

Exact repair state and whether the ship reaches Coconut Beach before the fork collision remain unresolved.

### Command Claim

Mr. Plugg claims command. Master Scourge enforces that claim.

Their authority is coercive and not fully legitimate.

### Physical Priorities

- Repair hull damage enough to sail or reposition.
- Operate bilge pumps and prevent further flooding.
- Refill fresh water.
- Gather food.
- Rendezvous with the supply effort at Coconut Beach.
- Maintain enough sailors to move the ship.
- Prevent open mutiny before Plugg can eliminate opposition.
