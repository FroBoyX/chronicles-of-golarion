## Raw Narrative

> We went south to the cove where we saw the Grindylow’s with Sandara’s hat and saw that it was a tidal pool with a cave that had a current coming in and another cave where it was flowing out. We started climbing down to the cove when some Stirge’s that nested nearby flew out to attack us. We all ended up diving into the water and swam in the cave entrance that water was flowing out and where the currents weren’t so crazy. This turned out to be where the Grindylows were hanging out, including the one wearing Sandara’s hat. Harvus killed one of the Grindylows while I went towards the cave entrance after seeing Sho get ambushed by 4 more Grindylows. She took out two of them before going down, I took out the other 2 and pulled Sho back to the surface before handing her off to Mirei. Harvus began to move further into the cave to chase after the Grindylow’s while I am guarding the entrance to make sure nothing ambushes from behind (and because I don’t have darkvision so I can’t see inside the cave, it’s dark).
>
> Things were starting to look a little iffy in the cave with the Grindylows swarming us but we managed to pull a victory out of our asses. I heard a lot of fighting coming from within the cave but…it was dark and I couldn’t see.
> Eventually Sho and Mirei came running towards the entrance saying to retreat (I guess things were getting pretty bad in there). Mirei touched my pants and things grew brighter but it also revealed a few Grindylows that were chasing them towards the entrance. I stepped in and saved the day once again.
> I hadn’t seen Harvus for a bit and the other 2 mentioned he had run further in so, with my bright ass pants, I ventured further in to see where he had gone. After rounding the corner another Grindylow had the balls to try and attack me, he is now a pool of blood. Harvus ended up being near there and ended up passing out as we were exiting the cave. I grabbed him and swam out through the other cave to the open ocean, once again saving my team. It ain’t easy being the hero all the time.
> After climbing up the cliff and retrieving my rope (with another refreshing swim through the water to avoid the Stirges) we all ended up back on shore. I did manage to spot what looked like the wreckage of another ship a couple miles into the sea but I didn’t feel like investigating it at the time. We can always come back later and it was starting to get close to when the ship would leave us so we needed to get back soon.
> After heading back to the camp, refilling my waterskin with some delicious water outside the stronghold on the way, I grabbed the coconuts and we headed back towards the boat. When we got to the fork I decided to use my spiffy new spyglass to try and see down the path we had not gone. After traveling a tiny bit I was able to see it was a very overgrown field of corn, that could prove useful later.
> We then went back to the great tree fire and camped for the night. I spent some of my time on watch brewing a potion from the sparse ingredients I had managed to find on this island since some of the others looked pretty beat up. In the morning we decided to split up, I would go with Sho to the boat and report what we had found while Mirei goes with Harvus to finish off the cave. That guy seems pretty obsessed on clearing that place, seems to have a big crush on Sandara.
> After getting back to the river, I epically tossed most of the coconuts 70 feet across to the other side. After that we used the branches to climb over, recollect them and continue down to the village we had landed at. Pretty uneventful journey there, after we arrived we saw that a bunch of the crew from the Man’s Promise were on shore already and searching through the village for stuff.
>
> I approached the village with Sho and we were approached by the crew members we saw. They were being overseen by Scourge who immediately asked where the other two (Harvus and Mirei) were. I informed them that they were cleaning up a Grindylow nest we had found which apparently pissed Scourge off. After letting him know we found food and water, he mumbled something (I wasn’t listening, heard something about “minds of our own”) before having everyone attack us.
> No one drew weapons so I figured he just wanted to rough us up a bit but getting hit sucks and I didn’t feel like getting hurt. I still ended up getting whacked pretty good in the face by Samms but she seemed to feel bad about having to do it to follow orders so I’ll let it slide this time. Eventually Scourge listened to reason and called them off of me but still beat up Sho for mouthing off while they were attacking. She really has a bad mouth and knows how to get in trouble.
> With all of that settled, Scourge wanted me to tell him everything that happened on the island. I decided to give him the edited version:
>
> - First day was spent just getting through the swamp, nothing of note happened. Climbed/hopped over a river with a broken bridge and made camp when it got late.
> - Second day we managed to get out of the swamp and saw a bunch of rotting corpses in a tent. Set fire to it since it looked like a breeding ground for disease and I needed to cleanse it, can probably still see the smoke in the distance. Harvus and Sho got heavily injured by swarms of botflies at the edge of the swamp, something to watch out for. We continued forth after tending to their wounds a bit until we ran into a beach with giant palm trees. This is where we found those coconuts but there were giant crabs living in some/most of them, I used my wits and led the other to trees that had no shells in order to safely get coconuts out. Who knows what would have happened if I wasn’t there. I made a glorious basket-thing from some leaves to drag some coconuts back with.After that we went back to trail and continued up hill till we found a stronghold. The stronghold was empty except for some weird vine creatures in a tree and some guy who hung himself in a shack. I wanted to set fire to it since he seemed a little undead but I didn’t have anything useful to start a fire with on me. There is also a spring of fresh water running out of the stronghold where I filled my waterskin from, that’s some delicioussss water and I recommend bringing back some barrels of it. I also spotted the Grindylows from this stronghold wearing some items that made me think they were the same ones to attack our ship during the storm. It was getting late by then so we camped for the night outside of the stronghold before going to find out if these Grindylows were the same that attacked us.
> - The third day was spent making our way to the Grindylow nest and investigating it. We ended up having to fight them off and killed a bunch of them before I had everyone get back out to regroup and report what they’d seen. It was also here that I spotted the sunken ship a few miles from shore on the other side of the island. After tallying up how many Grindylows were killed and how many might be left, Harvus and Mirei were confident they could finish searching the caves and dealing with any Grindylows they encountered on their own. I knew we were running out of supplies so I wanted to get back and report the food and water locations and came back, Sho followed while I left Mirei and Harvus to deal with the caves. On the way back I stopped by some fields we saw (they were down a different path) and found out it was full of corn. I didn’t want to spend too long checking it out and so I let it be for the time being to come back here.
> - Today was spent making our way back to here before the ship left. Harvus and Mirei are likely still investigating the caves for anything missing from the ship after the attack while Sho and I trekked back through the jungle and swamp while dragging the coconuts to bring word of what we found.
>
