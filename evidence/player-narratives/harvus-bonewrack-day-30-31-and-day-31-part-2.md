## Raw Narrative

### Day 30, This Damn Island

#### Harvus POV

The snake hath revealed a cove southward. Lo! A grindylow bears the hat of Sandara ’pon its pate. No lowly creature shall defile her in such manner. Revenge!

We set forth through the rugged jungle and slopes of the ridge to the entrance of the cove. Tis some 40-60ft down. Rak furnishes me with his climbing equipment and I set on making a way down.

Upon reaching bottom, “Sandara!”…. no answer…. save from a group of stirges nesting in the cove….. “not good.” One finds purchase upon my shoulders. “Vile creature.” I submerge and it releases. Inside the cave it is dark. 11 (by my count) grindylows spring forth and begin advancing. Rak furnishes me with a spear, to which I impale one on.

What news from the south doth my ears receive? Hark! The monk hath fallen in battle as more creatures attempt a flank…. yet the snake denies them such bold action. His courage is to be commended, performing such a… dance?.. in the midst of battle whilst retrieving a comrade from the throngs of death.

Now these creatures shall know my wrath…

Session ended with me deciding to head into the caves… alone… because that will work out well for me. Right?

### Day 30, This Damn Island (pt 2)

*I will find Sandara. And the plague of vile creatures here within will rue this day!* **Carefully tread deeper into the caverns that awaits, spear in hand. A pack of grindylows surface and attempt to ambush. One bears Sandara’s hat.** You, wretched thing, I will end you last. For the rest? I remove you from your mortal coil.

**Use hero point to enter “rage”. Kill a few. The grindylow bearing Sandara’s hat flees. A couple implant their spears into me. One more so than the other (got crit).** *I will be no easy-lad for the whims of these creatures.* **Withdraw from combat to a nearby tunnel. Drink a potion to heal up. Two of the grindylows follow suit. Kill one. The previous that crit me, hits me again.** *I’ve had enough of you.* **Gore the grindylow with my spear and slam its corpse against the walls in a deep fit of rage.**

*FUCK. Tis seeming that only my rage bears me. I must retreat…. rest.* Calistria grant me strength to return and smite these low things with glorious vengeance. *Trust my love that I will find you and deliver you from this place!* **Retreat through the tunnel back to the entrance. Come up to Rak.** *Doth my eyes deceive me? Or hath the snake found luminescent breeches? It matters not*

Rak, we must retreat for the time. And further more, you may need to bear me. **Rage ends. HP drops to the negs. Black out.**

**Wake up sometime later on a cliff over looking the ocean.** How came here did I? **Rak regales me with tales of rope acquisition and cliff scaling with me on his back.** Rak Wraithriser. I am much in your debt. And I will see that repaid.

**We hike back up the ridge, past the stockade, and back down the path towards the beach. We reach a junction, Rak looks down the path with a spyglass and discerns a wild grown field of corn.** The sun will be setting soon. If we tarry too long, we’ll be setting camp in the dark. **Rak suggests camping by the bonfire we set that still rages. I concur. Sho shakes her head.**

**We camp for the night.**

**Session ends with the party splitting in the morning. Because that always works out well for parties. Right?**

### Day 31: Once more unto the breach

**A night of rest. HP isn’t completely crap. Still bear signs of trauma.** *No longer I shall tarry in my duty. My state must suffice*

**Rak, Sho, and Mirei wish to head to the ship and report our findings on food and water.** I cannot go with you. I will not leave Sandara, alive or dead, in the clutches of those monsters. I will deliver her from that doom. If I must, I will return for her alone. But return I will. **I begin my trek back to the caves. Mirei volunteers to accompany me.**

*Calistria, hath thou answered mine prayers? Hath thou giveth me strength of rage this day? …I feel it, goddess. And I will carry it to the end. Vengeance upon those that would harm her thus! Vengeance to those whom forsook us to this hell.* **Mirei and I circumvent the ridge and return to the cove from the sea entrance. Four creatures pose as sentinels to their layer.**

*Your end is nigh*

**Climb up the cliff. Position atop one grindylow. Drop down, spear first. The others, unsure, look on as a black ink permeates water.**

*One.*

**My spear emerges from the ink to gore another**

*Two.*

**The two remaining take up posts just outside the tunnel entrance and ready actions. I surface in front of them triggering their actions. One misses, but not the other. I respond in kind**

*Three.*

**The final makes its stand fruitlessly.**

*Four.*

**With the tunnel clear, Mirei and I make our way back inside stealthily.** *Should have entered at low tide before. Would have been in our favor.* **The tunnels are eerily empty.** *Guess we thinned the population a bit.* **We come to a chamber with 3 possible exits. The sounds of creatures can be heard from only one however**

Mirei, pause here, I will scout ahead and return with news. **She hands me a potion of invisibility. I thank her, knock it back, and scout the tunnel. It ends in a large, flooded chamber. The water is littered with debris, most of it corpses of some kind or another. Three creatures inhabit the chamber. The grindylow wearing Sandara’s hat, an angry female grindylow, and a large whale like thing. Above, dangling from seaweed and sinew….**

*Now is the winter of my discontent, made glorious summer by Besmara’s own. I have found you! Now to free you.* **With her also is Tilly Brackett and someone I recognize from the Man’s Promise.** *Humans…. better to have you as allies than to wind up your corpse. You too will come with as well.*

**Sneak back to Mirei, get her to cast Cure L. Wounds on me. Suddenly 3 splooshes in water can be heard followed by commotion from the chamber. I sneak back.**

**Player aside: Somehow, even like… 60 god damn feet away, with cave ambiance, and all the racket the creatures are making in the chamber, somehow they can still hear Mirei cast her little spell. I guess spellcasters can’t cast without fucking screaming at the top of their lungs. Shenayays homie, shenaynays. The DC for that perception check should have been like… I don’t know… higher than it was. Just sayin… Ok that’s the end of my rant… because what follows turned out pretty epic.**

**Back at the chamber** *Shit! They’re in the drink. Alas, we’re discovered. So much for that advantage.* **Sneak into the water. Begin swimming after the my ship mates. The whale swims right above me, narrowly missing me. Soon I pass a large tentacle thing with teeth everywhere (a devilfish).**

*Really?*

**Finally catch up with them at the bottom. A pair of shackled ghouls await. One swipes at me and lands its hit** *Doth this forsaken gape not bear enough yet?*

**Pat Tilly on the shoulder as a sign of benevolence, cut her loose, and hand her my flail. She blindly swims to the surface. Cut Sandara free and place her on my shoulders, then cut Goyle Free. Begin swimming back up.**

*I must succeed.*

**About half way, the whale makes a lunge for Sandara (being that I’m still invisible. Use a hero point to brace my spear against its attack. It attempts to swallow us whole…. Rage on…. I crit the monster, stabbing it repeatedly from the inside. Its jaws land a few deep bites in my flesh.**

*No more shall they rend thy flesh.* **I shield Sandara from the bites, taking the all the damage. I do however survive it and keep going, watching as the devil fish blows bast and begins feasting upon the whale’s corpse.** *Enjoy creature. And toil not with us.*

**As I continue my way up, I notice Tilly embroiled in… something… with a small squid monster.** *Guess I ought deal with thine troubles too.* **Spear it. Kill it. Now I have to clutch my spear in my mouth, Sandara on my shoulders, and an unconscious Tilly Brackett with a dead squid stuck to her. Mirei calls out to me through a message spell she had cast prior to my entering the water. She asks me to gurgle if I’m still alive.**

Gurgle, gurgle…. **lose the spear…. because god forbid I should be able to gurgle with a spear in my mouth. Apparently one can only make noises when their mouth is empty.**

**Attempt to grab it with my feet. Fail.** *Fantastic. And it would appear that Tilly hath lost MY weapon….sigh* **Surface. No other creatures in sight. Mirei helps me get Sandara and Tilly above water.**

Session ends with Mirei and I looking over Sandara and Tilly, wondering where Goyle is.

### Arodus 5th, 4701 (Day 31 pt. 2): Onward we press

Check for signs of life in Sandara. She coughs water. She lives! Mine soul be still. I roll her on her side to allow for the water to exit properly. Tilly mentions Sandara taking a beating from the grinylows. Have you naught means to heal her by chance?. She denies means, then promptly collapses from wounds sustained by the squid. I catch her and lend support. She inquires into Mirei and I’s presence. We found sign of Sandara’s presence here. I, Mirei, Rak and the monk ’tempted rescue yesterday, but were driven back by the creatures within and forced to try again today. We returned while the other two sought to return and report.

Tilly mentions Goyle’s absence. He weren’t but a bit behind. I fear Davy Jones hath taken him. She suggests looking for him. I hesitantly concede and climb back in the waters. Goyle then surfaces, removing a piece of tentacle from his arm. I help him ashore. He regales me with tales of duplicity, corruption, and betrayals yet to happen. Mirei admits to being charged with killing Rak, Sho and I. A flash of rage surges though me momentarily. However, knowing my life was literally in her hands not long ago, and still having it, soothes that rage. There’s talk that Rak and Sho could be dead already. Goyle suggests we leave immediately.

Agreed. Let us make haste anon.

We depart the caves. I carry Sandara, while Goyle takes care of Tilly. We swim our way back to the shore near the cornfield. Once there, we rest momentarily before set off again. The group decides to circumvent the cornfield, not knowing what dangers reside within. Finally we make it around and continue on the path towards where we are to rendezvous with the others.

Session ends with us near a fork in the path and faint voices are heard in the distance.

## Date-Lock Impact

The heading `Arodus 5th, 4701 (Day 31 pt. 2)` cannot be treated as reliable objective date metadata:

- it labels the scene as **Day 31 part 2**, directly continuing the Day 31 rescue;
- the body says the failed rescue occurred `yesterday` and the successful return occurred `today`;
- `4701` is not the campaign year, which is 4712 AR;
- the heading therefore contains at least one definite metadata error and most likely two.

Harvus' narrative preserves the consecutive relative sequence:

- Day 28 = landing and first camp;
- Day 29 = swamp / swarm / beach travel;

With the grounding and landing locked to Arodus 1, Day 31 maps to **Arodus 4, 4712 AR**.

This independently matches:

- the three-rest GM clarification.

The objective chronology is therefore locked as **Arodus 4, 4712 AR** for the final-session branches. Preserve `Arodus 5th` and `4701` only as historical source labels or quoted errors.
