## Raw Narrative

### Day 28 (I think)

#### Sho's P.O.V.

I was excited to see that we were going to be spending time off of the ship and away from those despicable and rotten excuses of people Plugg and Scourge. The nights prior were quite draining and hectic. I found it difficult to try and say anything to Harvus about Sandara, and Mirei had seemingly closed herself off to me. I suspect that Scourge is keeping a close eye on her at the moment so I decide to keep silent about the matter.

We arrive on the island with little trouble thankfully, though the place feels as though it has a story to tell. Had the circumstances been different, it might have been an interesting place to explore. We made our way to a destroyed bridge across a swampy river. Most of us climbed, but Rak, never one to miss a chance to “Parkour” as he puts it, jumped across the remaining stumps to cross. To his credit he nearly made it, and the only thing that stopped him was a large frog monster. I was about to leap into the fray, but Mirei surprised me with a display of magic, entrancing the frogs long enough for Rak to get loose and escape.

We soon found a suitable clearing with which to call it a night and set up watches around a fire so as to not get caught unaware. Which thankfully we did as we were ambushed in the night by three undead women. It was over quickly, though I do believe one of the boys were bit. It didn’t seem to affect them, though still I worried a little. The night passed rather quickly after that and I hoped the next day would fare better.

### Day 29 (Im fairly certain)

#### This island hates us

#### Sho's P.O.V.

This next day on the island I had hoped would go better, but would soon find this island would become quite the ordeal to overcome. We traveled through rather thick jungle, with the path the only real indication of where we were going. Rak seemed to be looking for anything he could use in his alchemical concoctions with the other two and myself staying mostly silent. We came across what I originally hoped to be a clearing, but found it instead to be swampy marshlands. The place had a feeling of foreboding, which quickly grew with the sound of buzzing as we moved further in.

Rak had the right idea when he heard the buzzing grow louder as he quickly handed the few bottles of alchemist fire he carried. We were shortly set upon by disgusting swarms of some kind of mosquitoes. We were barely able to fend off one wave, myself and Harvus taking quite a nasty blow from their bites. (EWWW EWWWW EWWW GET THEM OFF!)

We heard another group of them fast approaching and admittedly in a blind panic I dove into the swamp water in the hopes to escape. The route I took unfortunately didn’t go forward but went back the way we came. At the time however, I was not in the soundest of mind to care, I only desired to get away from the swarm. As I made it back I could hear no sign of the mosquitoes, but I also saw no sign of the group. I cursed myself for panicking the way I did and after a few minutes of waiting, psyched myself up to take another dip into the swamp to find the others.

I swam for what seemed like a while, poking my head up only to get my barrings and for air. However, as I got closer, I could smell smoke, and soon saw the blaze of a fire on the horizon. I grew concerned something went wrong and when I heard no sign of the buzzing, jumped back onto land and took off in a sprint. I arrived to find that the fire had been started by none other than my group to which I asked justifiably what the hell had happened, the only response I got was from Rak, who looked at me with a smile and shaking his hands in excitement said “ALCHEMY! SSSSSSS….” A few small explosions erupted from the fire and I figured I wouldn’t be getting a straight answer from anyone that day.

Harvus seemed to have taken a larger hit than I realized from the mosquitoes’ assault and so I parted with one of my two potions of cure light wounds, deciding it best if I drink the other. After a moment of rest we pressed onward, we didn’t have a lot of time before the boat would leave us so we needed to find what we could.

We ended up finding a beach just east of the path. We noticed a figure off in the distance near a giant palm tree and decided to investigate. There were many giant palm trees that dotted the beach, some with smashed coconuts underneath them. When we drew close enough to see the figure in detail, it was obvious they were undead, however they seemed content with smacking the base of one of the larger palm trees. Before we had a chance to do anything however, and much to our surprise, a giant crab dropped from the tops of the tree and flattened the undead in the blink of an eye.

We decided we were in no shape to fight such a foe and backed away quickly, however the coconuts in the other trees seemed like quite the haul we needed. Both Harvus and I climbed to the top of one of the trees. I threw down coconuts whilst Harvus chopped some of the leaves so that Rak could makeshift construct a basket to hold these giant coconuts in. False dusk began to set in as we made our way back to the road, and in the hopes to gleam more from the island, as well as be in a better position we hiked upward on the rocky slopes of the path and finally made camp for that night. Boy do I hope the worst is behind us here on the island.

Honestly I feel like I might have had Rak all wrong, his violence on the mans promise had me worried, but his desire to help out all of us leads me to believe he has quite the kind side as well.

Once at the top, we rested a few minutes more. Harvus began to come to, and given our current situation, we decided to make a retreat for now, with the intent to come back sometime after we contacted the crew of the ship. We made it all the way back to the blazing fire the others had created a couple of days prior and rested for the night.

### Day 31 (No one's correcting me so its gotta be right)

#### Scourge is gonna go down

#### Sho's P.O.V.

Upon awakening we spend a small amount of time getting ready. Rak looks as though hes come up with some new concoctions, Mirei looks as though shes prepared her magic more so than usual, and Harvus seems to have an even more intense look of determination than before. As for myself, I think I have a better idea on how to handle the elemental magic that flows through my blood. I had hoped that I wouldn’t have to test my theory to soon however.

Harvus turned to the group and in an unshakable tone said that he was going to head back to the cove and hopefully find Sandara alive. Part of me really wanted to help, but I felt as though I would be of little use down there. Just as I was resigned to offer my assistance anyway, Mirei spoke up, offering to assist the bold strix with his quest. Rak offered a potion to assist him, but we knew someone had to meet the crew of the ship lest we all be left behind. And so with the intent of convincing the others to come back for them, the party parted ways.

Rak thankfully had another potion available for use and at my request, handed it to me. I had no idea what we would encounter on the way back, but I’d rather be as best prepared for it as I could. We were safely able to make it to the destroyed bridge, where Rak showed off his skills and chucked all but five coconuts clearly across the river. We climbed the trees and branches back across and made our way to the beach we originally landed at with record time.

However when we arrived, we found that some members of the crew were already there, looking through the huts and bones that littered the beach-side. Among them was Scourge who demanded to know what happened on the island and the whereabouts of the other two. We explained the short version of the events that happened which then prompted him to command the others to beat us for “Not following orders”.

Slippery Syl was the first to hit me across the face whilst Ebony slipped in behind. I saw Rak hit with a rather hard thwap from Samms. I took up a defensive stance and made a threat that the next person to hit me would soon regret it. This didnt go over well with Scourge however, as he made another snide remark and whipped my face from a distance. Ebony kept attempting to grab and hold me from behind, but I continued to slip out of his grasp, and after a few more hits of both the physical and verbal, they offered me an ultimatum. Kneel and stop what I was doing and they would forgive, or else. I kneeled, but I think Ebony could sense my hostility and intent to go all out on Scourge the moment he got close enough. He looked to me and caught my eye and shook his head slowly, as though he were trying to tell me something. I clenched my fist and bit my lip and decided to trust him, both out of guilt, and something told me he knew more than he let on. Regardless I looked up at Scourge once again before getting struck one more time and then only blackness.
