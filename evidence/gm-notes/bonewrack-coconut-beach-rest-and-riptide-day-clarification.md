# GM Note — Bonewrack Coconut Beach Rest and Riptide Day Clarification

## Document Status

- Type: GM truth / chronology clarification
- Maturity: Canon-setting correction
- Scope: Post-swarm rest, Coconut Beach placement, stockade and first Riptide Cove assault sequencing, post-assault rest, and flexible long-rest timing
- Canon status: Use to revise the Arodus 1-4 memory spine unless later direct logs supersede a detail

## GM Clarification

- After the swamp and swarm damage, the party camped and took a long rest at **Coconut Beach**.
- This stop was caused by accumulated damage and the need to recover, not because ordinary nightfall forced the party to stop.
- The stockade / spring discovery and the first failed Riptide Cove assault occurred within the same later adventuring period or calendar day.
- After the failed assault, the party slept again at night.
- The long rests should not be reconstructed as automatic `10 PM to 6 AM` blocks. Their start and end times may slide according to damage, travel, encounter duration, and the small scale of Bonewrack Isle.

## Chronology Impact

This supports three distinct long-rest periods during the early island sequence:

1. **Initial island camp** after landing, with Mirei's hidden flask placement and the third-watch ghoul attack.
2. **Coconut Beach long rest** after the swamp / swarm sequence because the party was badly damaged.
3. **Post-Riptide long rest** after the first failed assault, with Harvus later waking near the cliff as darkness came on.

It does **not** support a separate rest after the stockade.

The stockade / spring and first Riptide Cove assault should be kept in the same active period between the Coconut Beach long rest and the post-assault long rest.

## Working Date Implication

A clean working mapping is now possible:

- **Arodus 1:** grounding, late landing, early frog / mire encounter, first camp, hidden flask, ghoul attack.
- **Arodus 2:** renewed travel, swarm damage, foul tree / rotting camp, arrival at Coconut Beach, injury-forced long rest.
- **Arodus 3:** departure after the Coconut Beach rest, stockade / spring / spyglass sequence, first Riptide Cove assault, retreat, and post-assault long rest.
- **Arodus 4:** party split after the rest; Rak and Sho return toward the Man's Promise while Harvus and Mirei return to Riptide Cove.

Exact clock times remain reconstruction rather than recovered fact.
