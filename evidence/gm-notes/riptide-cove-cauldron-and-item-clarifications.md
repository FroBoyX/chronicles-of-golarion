## Whale Encounter Order

The corrected encounter order is:

1. Harvus begins descending into the Cauldron, starting the encounter.
2. The Whale charges upward at Harvus from below.
3. Harvus braces Rak's spear against the charge.
4. Harvus scores an actual critical hit with the spear and deals triple damage with a strong damage roll, killing the Whale.
5. Harvus continues downward using the Whale's corpse as cover.
6. Harvus reaches and frees Tilly, Sandara, and Goyle.
7. Harvus begins the return ascent with the captives.
8. The devilfish feeds on the Whale's corpse instead of Harvus and the captives.
9. The remaining grindylows flee after the Whale dies.

The Whale therefore dies before Harvus frees the captives. The earlier player retrospective sequence that places the Whale attack during the captive ascent does not control objective encounter order.
