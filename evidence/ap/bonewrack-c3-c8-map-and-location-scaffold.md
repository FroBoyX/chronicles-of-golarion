# Bonewrack C3-C8 Map and Location Scaffold

## Document Status

- Type: Published Adventure Path scaffold summary
- Maturity: Supporting reference
- Scope: Abandoned fishing village, mire, Whores' Boudoir, Crab Palm Beach, fields, Castaway's Stockade, and nearby Infernus route geography
- Parent work: Bonewrack Island reconstruction
- Player-facing status: Not player-facing by default
- Use rule: Published geography, encounter populations, and treasure remain scaffold only. Played events and GM truth control.

## Island Map Geometry

- The island overview uses a scale of one square to approximately one-third mile.
- C3, the abandoned fishing village, lies on the northern beach.
- The established path runs inland from C3 to C4, the mire.
- C5, the Whores' Boudoir, lies farther south along the same northeastern route.
- C6, Crab Palm Beach, lies on the eastern coast below C5.
- C7, the overgrown fields, occupies the southeastern interior.
- C8, the Castaway's Stockade, lies southwest of the fields near the western ridge.
- Riptide Cove lies on the southwest shore beyond C8.
- C9, the wreck of the Infernus, lies offshore southwest of the island.

## C3 - Abandoned Fishing Village

- A group of collapsed mud huts occupies the northern beach.
- The village has been abandoned for decades.
- A path leads inland toward C4.

## C4 - The Mire

- A salt river and quicksand bog form a roughly 70-foot barrier.
- The old bridge has collapsed, leaving platforms at either bank and pilings through the mire.
- Crossing options include the pilings, overhanging branches, rope-assisted movement, or the western cliffs.
- Two giant frogs wait submerged and use their tongues to pull travelers into the mire.

## C5 - Whores' Boudoir

- A tent and rotting decorations surround a great tree in a fetid clearing.
- Three former Infernus prostitutes became ghouls and use the site as their lair.
- During the day they remain at the lair.
- At night they hunt along the island paths.
- AP treasure includes six flasks of alchemist's fire, a healing potion, daggers, perfume, clothing, gems, and coin.
- None of this treasure is campaign loot unless table evidence establishes recovery.

## C6 - Crab Palm Beach

- A long white-sand beach is lined with roughly 50-foot coconut palms.
- Three giant crabs occupy separate trees associated with smashed coconuts.
- Each crab attacks when prey passes beneath or disturbs its tree.
- The AP does not place an undead figure on this beach.

## C7 - Fields

- The overview map places the overgrown fields south of C6 and east of C8.
- The detailed field encounter is outside the supplied excerpt.

## C8a - Castaway's Stockade

- A sharpened timber stockade surrounds a small lodge and great tree.
- A spring beside the tree is the island's only published fresh-water source.
- A weathered spyglass is fixed to the wall and aimed at D1, Riptide Cove.
- Looking through it reveals two grindylows in the cove; one wears Sandara Quinn's tricorne.
- Two vine chokers hide in the tree.
- AP treasure in the upper branches includes a water-breathing potion and small valuables.

## C8b - Stockade Lodge

- The lodge contains furniture salvaged from the Infernus and rotting provisions.
- Arron Ivy hangs from a failed Chelish execution collar and has become a ghast.
- A ghoul-fever-bearing botfly swarm occupies the lodge around his body.
- AP treasure includes armor, spears, Chelish clothing, a ring of swimming, spices, and silver valuables.
- None of this treasure is campaign loot unless table evidence establishes recovery.

## C9 - Wreck of the Infernus

- The wreck lies offshore southwest of the island.
- The supplied excerpt provides map placement but not the full encounter text.
