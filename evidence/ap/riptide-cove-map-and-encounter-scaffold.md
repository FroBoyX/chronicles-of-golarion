- Type: Adventure Path scaffold summary
- Maturity: Preserved comparison source

- Scope: Published Riptide Cove geography, encounter populations, tactics, hazards, and item placements for comparison against played campaign evidence

## Map Areas

### D1 — Riptide Cove

- Cliff-ringed tidal cove.
- Cliffs vary roughly 40-80 feet.
- Stirges nest above the water at the marked cliff location.
- The AP activates four stirges at one time from a larger nest population.

### D2 — The Neck

- Long, always-flooded passage between the open sea and D1.
- Churning current and sharpened stone make it the more difficult swim route.
- Four grindylows watch the ocean-side entrance.
- Their published duty is sea watch rather than island-side cove watch.

### D3 — The Fronds

- Main lair entrance from D1.
- Deep seaweed and bone-jaw decorations.
- Eight grindylows defend the entrance and use coordinated swarm / flank / jet tactics.
- The AP places Sandara Quinn's tricorne and selected belongings here.

### D4 — The Mesh

- Small barred cavern.
- Two trapped aquatic ghouls / lacedons attack through or near the submerged bars.
- Contains a small AP treasure cache.

### D5 — The Cataract

- Multi-exit guard cavern.
- A devilfish guards the route toward D8.
- The devilfish can create concealment with its blood and may retreat to D8 when badly injured.

### D6 — The Drowning Cave

- Broad seaweed-filled cavern.
- Contains a falling spiked-grille trap in the published version.

### D7 — The Gullet

- Deep chamber with conflicting tides.
- Eight additional grindylows defend it using tactics similar to D3.
- Contains AP treasure including a wand and scrimshaw valuables.

### D8 — The Cauldron

- Deep central flooded chamber with extremely poor underwater visibility.
- Published inhabitants:
  - Brinebrood Queen;
  - the Whale;
  - two chained lacedons at the bottom.
- Published captives hang above the water and are dropped when intruders are detected.
- The Whale cannot leave D8 through the narrow tunnels.
- The Queen flees if the Whale dies.
- The Queen's summon preference is an octopus; the AP explicitly excludes summoning a squid.
- Published captive equipment lies scattered on the chamber floor.

## Published Item Placements Relevant to Campaign Reconstruction

- Sandara's tricorne begins in D3 in the AP scaffold.
- Other captive equipment is scattered in D8.
- The Queen carries a separate +1 returning harpoon.
- The AP gives Sandara a post-rescue tricorne reward beat, but that outcome is not automatically campaign canon.
- AP treasure in D4, D5, D7, and D8 is not recovered in campaign canon unless table evidence says the PCs searched or collected it.

## Preserved VTT Map-State Observations

The GM-supplied VTT screenshot aligns closely with the clean AP map.

Clear visual matches include:

- four active stirge tokens at the D1 cliff location;
- four grindylow tokens at the D2 ocean entrance;
- a dense grindylow group at the D3 entrance;
- creature tokens remaining elsewhere in D4 / D7 / D8;
- a large Whale token and clustered actors in D8.

The screenshot also contains an orange movement-ruler path extending eastward from the D8 cluster through the D5-side passage. The actor associated with that ruler is not preserved in the screenshot. Do not use it alone to establish the Queen's escape route, Harvus's movement, or another specific actor's path.

Token presence in the screenshot does not by itself prove survival. Tokens may represent setup, hidden actors, defeated actors left on-map, or current actors.

## Comparison Rules

Use the AP to resolve:

- physical connectivity;
- area identity;
- baseline creature placement;
- published tactics that match player-described mechanics;
- likely identity of unnamed creatures.

Do not use the AP to overwrite:

- campaign-added third captive Goyle;
- exact casualties;
- actual combat sequence;
- player positions unsupported by table evidence;
- item recovery;
- Queen visibility by individual characters;
- campaign-specific spell, weapon, or retreat details.
