## Raw Transcription

### GM + Harvus — Scourge / Rage / Calistria

Since discovering the culpability of Master Scourge for his “induction” to the crew of the Wormwood, as well as the sundering of his wing, rage has burned within Harvus the likes of which he hasn’t felt since the day his village was razed. To keep from succumbing to the blood lust he feels, he bides his time by quietly praying to Calistria for the chance to obtain proper revenge. When that day comes, no one will stand in the way of honoring his goddess.

### GM + Harvus — Gentle Repose / Mouth / Quent

Though less a secret and more a subtle trait that has gone unnoticed since his time aboard the Wormwood, Harvus’ teeth and gums have remained perfectly healthy despite an inability to maintain proper oral hygiene. This is attributed to the fact that, during his days an enforcer, Harvus was able to have Gentle Repose permanently cast on his mouth.

Harvus had a job that took him to Quent on Motaku Isle. While there he visited the House of Stolen Kisses and had one of the priestesses cast the spell on him. He then happened upon a wizard who was seeking guidance from Calistria. Harvus offered assistance to the wizard in exchange for making the gentle repose cast on him permanent. The wizard accepted and Harvus delivered.

Thus, his pearly whites will endure. Downside: Anyone who casts detect magic will pick up a necromantic aura from him.
