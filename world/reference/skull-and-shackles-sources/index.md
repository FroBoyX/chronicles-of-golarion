# Skull & Shackles Extended Source Catalog

## Status and Scope

- **Type:** External published-source catalog
- **Maturity:** Bibliographically verified; content unharvested
- **Catalog date:** 2026-07-24
- **Provenance:** User-supplied transcription of a Reddit recommendation post; original URL and author not supplied
- **Intended use:** Future Chronicle research, setting enrichment, encounter design, NPC/location scaffolding, and relaunch support
- **Canon status:** Reference only; published material never overrides table evidence or GM truth
- **Ownership:** The six main *Skull & Shackles* Adventure Path volumes are recorded as owned, format unverified. All other items are assumed not owned until checked.
- **Rights boundary:** Preserve metadata and Chronicle-oriented synthesis only. Do not reproduce substantial copyrighted prose, maps, art, stat blocks, or tables.
- **Harvest state:** Every entry remains unharvested. Reported contents come from the Reddit list unless a note explicitly says a public product description confirmed them.

## Verification Standard

Every named purchasable or downloadable product was checked for existence against publisher catalogs, publisher announcements, or reliable bibliographic records. The year is the first release year for the named edition or product. No page numbers have been invented. Page-level extraction waits for confirmed lawful access.

## Normalization Notes

- “Gloomspire Saga” is a community umbrella for five separate Pathfinder Society scenarios.
- Corrected **Hrenthar’s Throne** to **Hrethnar’s Throne** and standardized Paizo’s **On Sevenfingers’s Sails**.
- *Scions of the Sky Key*, *Champion’s Chalice*, and *Passing the Torch* are multi-scenario arcs expanded into their component products.
- *Beyond Azlant Ridge* is a later sequel to the two-part *Before the Dawn* arc, not its formal third installment.
- “Official alternate Book 2” is a community use-case for *Plunder & Peril*, not its formal designation.
- Ranzak is a separate 2014 Adventure Card Game promo character.
- PF2e, 3.5-era, d20, and card-game products are labeled so conversion needs remain visible.

## Harvest Tiers

- **A — Direct:** Shackles-specific or campaign-direct.
- **B — Adjacent:** Strong regional or thematic support.
- **C — Isolated:** A limited NPC, item, trait, artifact, or cross-campaign element.
- **D — Mention only:** Do not acquire solely for this connection.

## Future Harvest Record

When a source becomes lawfully available, record:

- format and ownership/access confirmation;
- edition or printing;
- exact relevant pages or sections;
- a concise original summary;
- affected Chronicle entities and files;
- contradictions with campaign evidence;
- conversion needs;
- harvest date and reviewer.

Do not silently promote a Reddit recommendation into source-confirmed fact. Preserve the distinction between bibliographic verification, reported relevance, inspected source content, and adopted campaign material.
