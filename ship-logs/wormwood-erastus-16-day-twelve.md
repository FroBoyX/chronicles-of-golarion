**Ship's Log - The Wormwood**  
*Erastus 16, 4712 AR*

Land was sighted from the rigging during the morning watch. Sho Astor was released from overnight restraint at the mast when summoned for duty.

Mr. Plugg assigned Harvus G'ron, Rak Wraithraiser, Sho Astor, Mirei, and Badger Medlar to swim to the reef and collect crabs for the captain's table. The Wormwood anchored while the party completed the task.

The assigned hands returned with their catches after encountering reefclaws among the coral. Several crew members returned injured. The catch was delivered to the galley for preparation.

Rak Wraithraiser was credited with bringing back the largest reefclaw and was presented to Captain Harrigan. Harrigan ordered the quartermaster to return confiscated property belonging to Wraithraiser as a reward for useful service.

Sandara Quinn attended injured crew later that night. Ordinary night watches continued, and the Wormwood remained under normal shipboard routine.
