## Log Entry

**Erastus 15, 4712 AR**  
**The Untouched Cup**

Weather normal. Ordinary watches and assigned work proceeded without significant incident during the day.

At evening mess, Sho Astor refused the issued rum ration and openly challenged the order to drink. Master Scourge deferred punishment until Bloody Hour.

At Bloody Hour, Astor was secured to the main mast and whipped for refusal of the rum ration and insubordination. She was ordered left secured at the mast through the night.

The night watch relieved the day watch as scheduled. Astor remained at the mast at the close of the watch period. The Wormwood continued on course under normal sailing conditions.
